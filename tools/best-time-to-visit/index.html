Best Time to Visit

Compare seasons across every destination in our guides — weather, crowds and price.

  1. Home
  2. Tools
  3. Best Time to Visit
DestinationCountryBest monthsDays needed
KyotoJapan Late March to mid-April for cherry blossom — beautiful, and the busiest weeks of the year. 4–5 days for a first visit Guide
Hoi AnVietnam February to April 3–4 days, or a week if you are using it as a slow-travel base. Guide
Chiang MaiThailand November to February 4 days for the city and one mountain day Guide
UbudIndonesia April to October is the dry season and the reliable window. 4–5 days in Ubud, ideally within a 10–14 day Bali trip that includes the coast. Guide
Amalfi CoastItaly May, early June, and late September are the best weeks 4–5 days, or 7 combined with Naples or Capri. Guide
LisbonPortugal April to June and September to October 3–4 days for the city, 6–7 with Sintra and the coast. Guide
Lauterbrunnen ValleySwitzerland Late June to September for high trails clear of snow. 3–4 days minimum Guide
AlUlaSaudi Arabia November to March is the practical window; days are mild and nights cold. 3–4 days for the main sites without rushing. Guide
Wadi RumJordan March to May and September to November are ideal — warm days, cold nights. 1–2 nights. One night is the minimum to justify the journey Guide
MarrakechMorocco March to May and October to November 3–4 days for the city, 6–7 with the Atlas or a desert extension. Guide
Maasai MaraKenya July to October for the migration river crossings — the busiest and most expensive months. 3–4 nights. Two is too short to absorb a transfer day Guide
Banff & Lake LouiseCanada July to September for high trails, warm days and open teahouses. 4–5 days for Banff and Lake Louise Guide
OaxacaMexico October to April is dry and warm — the reliable window. 4–5 days for city and valleys Guide
El Chaltén & PatagoniaArgentina November to March is the southern summer trekking season. 4–5 days to allow for weather Guide
Cusco & the Sacred ValleyPeru May to September is the dry season and the main window. 5–7 days including acclimatisation, the valley and Machu Picchu. Guide
Queenstown & FiordlandNew Zealand December to March 4–5 days for Queenstown and Milford Guide
MunnarKerala September to March 3 days in Munnar Guide
UdaipurRajasthan October to March is the main season — warm days, cool nights. 3 days for the city Guide
Spiti ValleyHimachal Pradesh June to September is the main season, when both approach roads are open. 8–10 days minimum for the loop with proper acclimatisation. Guide
MeghalayaMeghalaya October to April is dry and clear — the main season. 6–7 days for the Khasi and Jaintia hills at a reasonable pace. Guide
