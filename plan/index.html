AI Trip Planner

Tell it how you travel. It builds the trip.

Destination, dates, budget, pace, interests and who is coming — and you get a day-by-day itinerary with stays, experiences, transport and a realistic budget attached to every day.

  1. Home
  2. Plan

How this planner works, honestly. Version one runs entirely in your browser: a deterministic matching engine over our own curated catalogue of 20 destinations, 27 stays and 31 experiences. It sends nothing to a server and stores nothing about you. It is designed as a drop-in for a language-model provider — see plannerProvider in assets/js/planner.js — so a live model can be connected without changing the interface or the funnel. It will never invent a hotel, a price or an availability.

01 Where and when

Pick a destination we cover, or let the planner suggest one from your interests.

02 Who is travelling

This changes the stays and the pace more than anything else you tell us.

03 Budget and standard

We plan in bands, not invented totals. Use the budget calculator for a number.

04 Interests

Pick as many as apply — these drive which experiences get scheduled.

Nothing is sent anywhere. Your answers stay in this browser tab.
