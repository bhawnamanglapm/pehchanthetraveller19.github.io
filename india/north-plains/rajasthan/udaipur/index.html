Udaipur

The lake city of Mewar — palace architecture, Aravalli hills and the most romantic urban setting in India.

Why visit Udaipur

Udaipur is built around a chain of artificial lakes constructed centuries ago, and the architecture is designed to be seen from the water. The City Palace complex is genuinely vast, the old city's havelis have been converted into some of India's best small hotels, and unlike much of Rajasthan the Aravalli hills keep it green. It is also the easiest Rajasthan city to travel slowly.

Best time to visit

  • October to March is the main season — warm days, cool nights.
  • November to February is ideal; December and January can be genuinely cold at night.
  • April to June is very hot, over 40°C, with lower prices.
  • The monsoon fills the lakes and is when the city looks its best, if you accept the rain.

How to get there

  • Udaipur (UDR) has direct flights from Delhi and Mumbai.
  • Overnight trains connect Delhi and Jaipur.
  • The old city is walkable; use autos or a car for the outer sights.

How many days do you need

3 days for the city; 10–12 for a Rajasthan circuit.

Where to stay

Lakeside havelis in the old city for atmosphere, hill palaces for views over the lakes, or Jagat and the Aravallis outside for rural luxury.

Best things to do in Udaipur

  • See the City Palace early, when the courtyards are still empty.
  • Take a boat on Lake Pichola in the last hour of light.
  • Visit Bagore ki Haveli's evening folk performance.
  • Drive out to the Ranakpur Jain temple and the Kumbhalgarh fort walls.
  • Walk the old city's craft lanes — miniature painting here is a living tradition.

Food

  • Dal baati churma, the Rajasthani staple.
  • Laal maas, a fiery Mewari mutton curry.
  • Kachori and mirchi bada from the old city stalls.
  • Rooftop dinners overlooking the lake — the most reliable pleasure in the city.

Safety & practical information

  • Generally very safe; standard city awareness applies.
  • Commission-based shop steering by auto drivers is common — be firm.
  • Summer heat is a genuine health risk; plan around midday.

Local culture

  • Remove shoes at temples and at some haveli sections.
  • Ask before photographing inside working temples.
  • Alcohol is available but discreet; several restaurants are vegetarian only.

Frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Udaipur?

Three is comfortable — the palace, the lake, the old city and one day trip to Ranakpur or Kumbhalgarh. Two works if you are moving quickly through Rajasthan.

Is Udaipur better than Jaipur?

They serve different purposes. Jaipur is bigger, has more monuments and better shopping; Udaipur is smaller, more atmospheric and much easier to travel slowly. Most Rajasthan trips include both.

When is the best time to visit Udaipur?

November to February for the most comfortable weather. September and early October are also excellent — the lakes are full from the monsoon and the crowds have not yet arrived.
