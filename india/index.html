India

Travel in India, region by region

Six regions, from the Char Dham shrines and Himalayan ridges to the Konkan coast and the southern backwaters.

  1. Home
  2. India

India does not travel like one country. The mountain states run on a season that opens and closes; the plains hold the densest pilgrimage routes anywhere; the coast and the south move at an entirely different pace. Pick the part of the country first — the itinerary follows from that.
