Can I do this?
List your business

Women-run businesses, listed free

If you run something travellers use — and you are a woman running it — Pehchan Local will list you at no charge.

  1. Home
  2. Pehchan Local
  3. List your business
What we list

Any service a traveller needs

Photographers, trek and walking guides, drivers, yoga and wellness, food experiences, salons, doctors and clinics, homestays — and anything else we have not thought of.

What it costs

Nothing, and no signal is for sale

Listing is free. Payment cannot buy a check mark and never will: the checks are the only reason a traveller trusts this page, so selling them would destroy the thing you are being listed on.

What we check

And what each check means

Phone verified
We called the number and spoke to her.
Met in person
Someone from Pehchan has met her at the destination.
Woman-run confirmed
We confirmed the business is owned or run by a woman.
Licence seen
We saw a current registration or licence for the work she does.
Travellers served
Women who booked through Pehchan and reported back.
Insured
We saw current liability cover.

Every check carries its date on your listing, and expires. We will come back to you before it does — a check that has quietly gone stale is worse than no check at all.

How to be listed

Tell us about your work

Email pehchanthetraveller@gmail.com with what you do, where you work, the languages you speak, and a number we can call. We call every applicant — that call is the first check.
