Luxury Rajasthan Escape

Udaipur, the Aravallis and the desert forts

Build This Trip See the days

Rajasthan
Style

$$$$ (indicative)
Best time
October to March
Travel time
Private car throughout; one internal flight

Overview

Eight days across southern Rajasthan at a pace that suits the heat and the architecture. Two bases rather than five, a private car, and the fort-and-temple days scheduled around the light rather than around a tour operator's clock.

Destinations on this route

Udaipur

Estimated budget

We publish budget bands rather than invented totals: this journey sits at $$$$ of four. To turn that into your own number — with your dates, your standard of stay and your flights — use the trip budget calculator.

Packing recommendations

  • Light cottons and linens; evenings in December are genuinely cold
  • Modest cover for temples
  • Sunglasses and a hat — the light is intense
  • A scarf, useful for temples, dust and air conditioning
  • Comfortable shoes for fort stairs
