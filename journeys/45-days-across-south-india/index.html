45 Days Across South India

Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and the coast

Build This Trip See the days

  2. Journeys

45 days
Countries
Kerala
Style
slow travel
Budget band
$$ (indicative)
Best time
October to March
Travel time
Trains and hired cars; no internal flights required

Overview

A long, deliberate journey through the four southern states, moving roughly every four to five days rather than daily. This is the itinerary for a sabbatical, a long leave or a first slow-travel experiment — the pace is the product.

Destinations on this route

Munnar

Estimated budget

We publish budget bands rather than invented totals: this journey sits at $$ of four. To turn that into your own number — with your dates, your standard of stay and your flights — use the trip budget calculator.

Packing recommendations

  • Light cotton throughout; one warm layer for the hill stations
  • A good daypack and a lockable main bag
  • Water purification — you will be refilling constantly
  • Modest clothing for temples across all four states
  • A rail booking account set up before you travel

AfternoonMattancherry, the spice warehouses and the synagogue.

TransportArrive Kochi (COK)

Munnar and the high ranges

MorningEstate walks at first light with plantation staff.
AfternoonEravikulam, Top Station, and the Kolukkumalai jeep track.
EveningVerandah evenings on a working estate.
Transport4 hours by road from Kochi
StayHigh Range Tea Bungalow — A planter's bungalow on a working estate
ExperienceKolukkumalai Sunrise Jeep — 5 hours

Days 11–15Thekkady and the backwaters

Thekkady and the backwaters

MorningSpice plantations around Kumily.
AfternoonDown to Alleppey; a night on a houseboat, then a homestay.
EveningBackwater villages by canoe rather than by houseboat.
Transport5 hours by road
Days 16–20Kerala coast

Kerala coast

MorningMarari or Varkala; the trip's first genuine rest.
AfternoonAyurvedic treatment courses run properly over several days.
EveningBeach and nothing else.
Transport2–3 hours by road or train
Days 21–26Tamil Nadu temple towns

Tamil Nadu temple towns

MorningMadurai's Meenakshi temple at the 5am opening.
AfternoonThanjavur and the Chola bronzes; Chettinad's mansions and cooking.
EveningLong train legs between towns — book sleeper class ahead.
TransportTrains, 3–6 hours between towns
Days 27–32Pondicherry and the Coromandel

Pondicherry and the Coromandel

MorningThe French quarter early, before the heat.
AfternoonMahabalipuram's shore temples and stone carving workshops.
EveningCoastal seafood and a slower rhythm again.
TransportTrain and road along the coast
Days 33–38Karnataka — Hampi

Karnataka — Hampi

MorningThe Vijayanagara ruins across the boulder landscape, on a bicycle.
AfternoonCoracle crossings and the Tungabhadra river.
EveningSunset from Matanga hill.
TransportOvernight train from Chennai or Bangalore
Days 39–45Coorg and the return

Coorg and the return

MorningCoffee estates in the Kodagu hills.
AfternoonMysore's palace and market on the way down.
EveningFinish in Bangalore or return to Kochi.
