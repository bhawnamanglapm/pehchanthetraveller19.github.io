14 Days in Thailand

Bangkok, the northern hills and the Andaman coast

Build This Trip See the days
  1. Home
  2. Journeys

Duration
14 days
Where
Thailand
Style
adventure
Budget band
$$ (indicative)
Best time
November to February
Travel time
Two internal flights, one overnight train

Overview

The classic Thailand shape done properly: a short city start, a full week in the north where the culture and the landscape are, and a coastal finish that is a rest rather than a second trip.

Destinations on this route

Chiang Mai

Estimated budget

We publish budget bands rather than invented totals: this journey sits at $$ of four. To turn that into your own number — with your dates, your standard of stay and your flights — use the trip budget calculator.

Packing recommendations

  • Light layers — the north is genuinely cool in December and January
  • Modest clothing for temples: shoulders and knees covered
  • Reef-safe sunscreen for the coast
  • A dry bag
  • Insect repellent for the hills

The itinerary

Days 1–3Bangkok

Bangkok

MorningWat Pho and the river ferries; skip the tuk-tuk touts entirely.
AfternoonChinatown food walking, then a rooftop for the scale of the city.
EveningOvernight sleeper train north, or fly.
TransportSleeper train to Chiang Mai, 12 hours
Days 4–6Chiang Mai old city

Chiang Mai old city

MorningDoi Suthep before sunrise, then the old city temples.
AfternoonNorthern Thai cooking class; the cuisine is not Bangkok's.
EveningFood trail through the old city and Chang Phueak.
TransportOn foot and by songthaew
StayOld City Teak House — A teak Lanna house inside the moat
ExperienceNorthern Thai Food Trail — 3.5 hours
Book the stay Book the experience
Day 7Elephants and hills

Elephants and hills

MorningFull day at an ethical sanctuary — no riding, no bathing.
AfternoonContinue north into the Mae Rim hills.
EveningCheck in above the coffee valleys.
Transport40 minutes north of the city
StayMae Rim Forest Lodge — Hill villas above the coffee valleys
ExperienceEthical Elephant Sanctuary Day — Full day
Book the stay Book the experience
Days 8–9Mae Rim and the Samoeng loop

Mae Rim and the Samoeng loop

MorningDrive or ride the Samoeng loop through the coffee hills.
AfternoonSpa afternoon, or the Mae Sa waterfall walk.
EveningDinner at the lodge; early night before the flight.
TransportHire car or driver for the loop
StayMae Rim Forest Lodge — Hill villas above the coffee valleys
Book the stay

Andaman coast

MorningFly south to Krabi or Phuket, then transfer to the coast.
AfternoonFour days of islands, longtail boats and doing very little.
EveningSunset on the beach; the trip's decompression.
TransportInternal flight, roughly 2 hours

MorningTransfer to the airport.
