Solo Vietnam Journey

Central Vietnam at a walking pace

Build This Trip See the days
  1. Home
  2. Journeys
  3. Solo Vietnam Journey
Duration
9 days
Where
Vietnam
Style
solo
Budget band
$$ (indicative)
Best time
February to April
Travel time
One internal flight, one coastal train

Overview

Nine days for a first solo trip to Vietnam, built around central Vietnam rather than the full north-to-south run. Small towns, short transfers, one place you stay long enough to have a regular coffee spot.

Destinations on this route

Hoi An

Estimated budget

We publish budget bands rather than invented totals: this journey sits at $$ of four. To turn that into your own number — with your dates, your standard of stay and your flights — use the trip budget calculator.

Packing recommendations

  • A bicycle-friendly daypack
  • Rain layer, even in the dry season
  • A padlock for guesthouse lockers
  • Small denomination cash for markets
  • A power bank for long transfer days
