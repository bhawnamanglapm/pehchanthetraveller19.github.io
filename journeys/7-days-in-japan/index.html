7 Days in Japan

Kyoto, Nara and the Kansai hills

Build This Trip See the days
  1. Home
  2. Journeys

Duration
7 days
Countries
Japan
Style
culture
Budget band
$$$ (indicative)
Best time
Late March to mid-April, or November
Travel time
Two internal transfers; everything else on foot or by local train

Overview

A first Japan trip that resists the Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka sprint. Seven days in Kansai, based almost entirely in Kyoto, with day trips out to Nara and the cedar hills. The argument is simple: one base, deep coverage, no packing and unpacking.

Destinations on this route

Kyoto

Estimated budget

We publish budget bands rather than invented totals: this journey sits at $$$ of four. To turn that into your own number — with your dates, your standard of stay and your flights — use the trip budget calculator.

Packing recommendations

  • Comfortable walking shoes you can slip off easily — you will remove them several times a day
  • A small folding bag for temple visits
  • Layers; Kyoto is colder in winter and more humid in summer than most visitors expect
  • A cash card that works at Japanese ATMs, plus some cash
  • An IC transit card, loaded on arrival
