European Summer Escape

Lisbon, the Alps and the Amalfi Coast by rail and air

Build This Trip See the days
  1. Home
  2. Journeys
  3. European Summer Escape
Duration
16 days
Where
Portugal, Switzerland, Italy
Style
slow travel
Budget band
$$$ (indicative)
Best time
June and September
Travel time
Two short flights, the rest by train

Overview

Sixteen days across three European climates — Atlantic city, alpine valley, Mediterranean coast — with enough time in each to stop moving. Built for June or September rather than August, and structured so that the two flights are the only stressful days.

Destinations on this route

LisbonLauterbrunnen ValleyAmalfi Coast

Estimated budget

We publish budget bands rather than invented totals: this journey sits at $$$ of four. To turn that into your own number — with your dates, your standard of stay and your flights — use the trip budget calculator.

Packing recommendations

  • One warm layer and a rain shell for the Alps, even in July
  • Swimwear for both the Atlantic and the Mediterranean
  • Walking shoes that work on both alpine trails and cobbles
  • A rail app for Swiss and Italian bookings
  • A packing cube system — you will unpack three times
