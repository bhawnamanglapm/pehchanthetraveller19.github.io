Himalayan Road Trip

Shimla to Spiti to Manali, the slow way

Build This Trip See the days
  1. Home
  2. Journeys
  3. Himalayan Road Trip
Duration
12 days
Where
Himachal Pradesh (Kinnaur & Spiti)
Style
adventure
Budget band
$$ (indicative)
Best time
June to September
Travel time
Long driving days on mountain roads; this is the trip

Overview

Twelve days across the Spiti circuit, entered from Shimla so that altitude is gained gradually and exited over Kunzum to Manali. The route is the itinerary — the driving days are not transfers between the good bits, they are the good bits.

Destinations on this route

Spiti Valley

Estimated budget

We publish budget bands rather than invented totals: this journey sits at $$ of four. To turn that into your own number — with your dates, your standard of stay and your flights — use the trip budget calculator.

Packing recommendations

  • Layers for a 25°C daytime and 0°C night swing
  • Diamox if your doctor advises it, plus a plan for altitude symptoms
  • Cash — ATMs are unreliable above Reckong Peo
  • A BSNL SIM, the only network with real coverage
  • Sun protection: UV at 4,000m is severe

Day by day

The itinerary

Days 1–2Shimla to Sangla

Shimla to Sangla

MorningDrive up through the Kinnaur valley along the Sutlej.
AfternoonSangla and the Baspa valley, around 2,700m.
EveningFirst night at moderate altitude — this is deliberate.
TransportRoughly 8 hours from Shimla
Days 3–4Kalpa and Nako

Kalpa and Nako

MorningKinnaur Kailash from Kalpa at sunrise.
AfternoonCross into the cold desert; the landscape changes completely at Nako.
EveningNako lake, 3,600m; sleep low if you feel unwell.
Transport6–7 hours of mountain driving
Days 5–6Tabo and Dhankar

Tabo and Dhankar

MorningTabo monastery, founded in 996, with its original murals.
AfternoonDhankar's cliff monastery and the walk to Dhankar lake.
EveningContinue to Kaza, the valley's main town.
Transport4–5 hours
Days 7–8Kaza and Key

Kaza and Key

MorningKey monastery for morning prayers.
AfternoonRest and acclimatise properly before going higher.
EveningKaza has the valley's only real services — restock here.
TransportShort local drives
ExperienceKey Monastery Morning Prayers — 3 hours
Book the experience
Days 9–10Langza, Komic and Hikkim

Langza, Komic and Hikkim

MorningUp to the high villages at 4,400m; fossil fields above Langza.
AfternoonHikkim's post office, among the highest in the world.
EveningVillage homestay, eating with the family.
Transport1–2 hours on rough roads
StayLangza Village Homestay — A community homestay at 4,400m
Book the stay
Day 11Chandratal and Kunzum

Chandratal and Kunzum

MorningOver the Kunzum pass at 4,550m, road conditions permitting.
AfternoonChandratal lake; camp only at the designated site.
EveningThe coldest night of the trip.
Transport5–6 hours, subject to road status
Day 12Down to Manali

Down to Manali

MorningThe descent through Batal and Gramphu — the roughest stretch of the route.
AfternoonArrive in Manali; the drop in altitude is immediately noticeable.
EveningA proper hot shower and a proper meal.
Transport8–10 hours, weather dependent
