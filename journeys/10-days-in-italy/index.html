10 Days in Italy

Naples, the Amalfi Coast and the islands

Build This Trip See the days
  1. Home
  2. Journeys

Duration
10 days
Where
Italy
Style
romantic
Budget band
$$$$ (indicative)
Best time
May, early June or late September
Travel time
One train, the rest by ferry and bus

Overview

Ten days built around the Amalfi coastline, travelled the way it works best — by ferry, from a single base, with two nights in Naples at the start for the food and the archaeology. No hire car, deliberately.

Destinations on this route

Amalfi Coast

Estimated budget

We publish budget bands rather than invented totals: this journey sits at $$$$ of four. To turn that into your own number — with your dates, your standard of stay and your flights — use the trip budget calculator.

Packing recommendations

  • Shoes with grip: this coast is built entirely from steps
  • A light layer for evening ferry crossings
  • Swimwear you can walk to the beach in
  • A dry bag for boat days
  • Modest cover-up for church visits

Days 1–2Naples

Naples

MorningArrive, drop bags, walk the Spaccanapoli.
AfternoonThe Archaeological Museum, which holds everything removed from Pompeii.
EveningPizza in the historic centre, then a walk along the seafront.
TransportNaples airport to the centre, 20 minutes
Day 3To Praiano

To Praiano

MorningTrain or transfer to Sorrento, then ferry along the coast.
AfternoonCheck in; swim at Marina di Praia.
EveningDinner on the terrace as the coast lights come on.
TransportFerry from Sorrento, roughly an hour
StayPraiano Lemon Terrace — Fourteen rooms on a working lemon terrace
Book the stay

Path of the Gods

MorningBus up to Bomerano and walk the ridge path westward.
AfternoonThe long stair descent into Positano; swim, then ferry back.
EveningEarly dinner — this is a tiring day.
TransportBus up, ferry back
StayPraiano Lemon Terrace — Fourteen rooms on a working lemon terrace
ExperiencePath of the Gods Guided Walk — 5 hours
