Why this exists. After the trip, someone will ask what it actually cost, what you wish you had known, and how you felt before you left. The last of those cannot be answered later — by the time you are home you will remember it wrong. So Part 1 is answered before you go. Andaman signal is patchy, so this is on paper on purpose.
