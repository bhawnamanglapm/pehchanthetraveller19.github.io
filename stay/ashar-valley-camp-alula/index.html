AlUla · Saudi Arabia

Ashar Valley Camp

Sandstone-canyon villas with private terraces

Overview

Twenty-four villas set against a sandstone wall in a side canyon of the Ashar valley, each with a private terrace facing the rock. Nights are for the sky; days are for Hegra, the oasis and the old town, all within twenty minutes.

Location

Ashar valley, AlUla — 20 minutes from Hegra and the old town, 25 from the airport.

Why stay here

  • Waking up against a two-hundred-metre sandstone wall changes the scale of the trip.
  • Close to every major AlUla site without being in the town.
  • Dark skies here are exceptional and the property runs its own stargazing.
  • Villa privacy with full resort service.

Best rooms

  • Canyon-wall villas at the far end of the property, furthest from the restaurant.
  • Pool villas for longer stays.
  • Entry villas are closer to facilities and lose some of the seclusion.

Amenities

Private terracesPoolStargazing programmeHegra tour bookingOasis cyclingAll-day dining

Who it is best for

  • Couples
  • Photographers
  • Travellers making AlUla the centre of a Saudi trip

Who it is not for

  • Budget travellers
  • Anyone visiting between May and September

Best time to visit

November to March. December to February for the cultural season.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Extraordinary geological setting
  • Genuinely dark skies
  • Close to all major sites

Considerations

  • Premium pricing
  • Remote — a car or shuttles are essential
  • No alcohol, in line with national law

Nearby attractions

  • Hegra — 20 min drive
  • AlUla old town — 20 min drive
  • Elephant Rock — 15 min drive
  • Gharameel stargazing field — 35 min drive
