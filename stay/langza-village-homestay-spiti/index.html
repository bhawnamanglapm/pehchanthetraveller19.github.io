Langza Village Homestay

A community homestay at 4,400m

Overview

Four rooms in a family house in Langza, part of Spiti's community homestay network, at around 4,400m. Rooms are simple and warm, meals are eaten with the family, and the income goes directly to the village rota rather than to an operator.

Location

Langza village, Spiti Valley — 45 minutes above Kaza.

Why stay here

  • The community homestay network is the best way to stay in Spiti, ethically and experientially.
  • Langza sits under Chau Chau Kang Nilda with fossil fields on the slopes above.
  • Eating with the family is the actual experience, not an add-on.
  • Income rotates between village households by agreement.

Best rooms

  • The two upper rooms with valley-facing windows.
  • Rooms are simple throughout — this is a family house, not a hotel.

Amenities

Home-cooked meals includedTraditional bukhari stove heatingFossil-field walksGuided village introductionsHot water by bucket

Who it is best for

  • Independent travellers
  • Slow travellers
  • Anyone wanting community-based tourism

Who it is not for

  • Travellers needing private bathrooms and constant power
  • Anyone not yet acclimatised — this is 4,400m

Best time to visit

June to September, when the roads are open.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Direct community benefit
  • Extraordinary setting
  • Very inexpensive

Considerations

  • Extreme altitude — acclimatise in Kaza first
  • Shared bathrooms and limited hot water
  • Intermittent electricity and no mobile signal

Nearby attractions

  • Komic village — 30 min drive
  • Hikkim post office — 20 min drive
  • Key monastery — 60 min drive
  • Kaza — 45 min drive
