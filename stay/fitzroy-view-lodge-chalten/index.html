El Chaltén & Patagonia · Argentina

Fitz Roy View Lodge

Eighteen rooms at the trailhead end of the village

Overview

A timber-and-glass lodge at the northern end of El Chaltén where the Fitz Roy trail begins, eighteen rooms with a drying room, a packed-breakfast service for early starts, and a common room built around the weather forecast.

Location

El Chaltén, Santa Cruz — at the northern trailhead, five minutes' walk from the village centre.

Why stay here

  • Being at the trailhead saves twenty minutes at the start and end of every walk.
  • Early breakfast packs and a drying room are the two things that actually matter here.
  • Fitz Roy is visible from the common room on clear mornings.
  • Staff track the weather windows, which in Patagonia is the whole game.

Best rooms

  • North-facing rooms with the Fitz Roy sightline.
  • Ground-floor rooms for boot access to the drying room.
  • Rear rooms are cheaper and face the hillside.

Amenities

Drying roomEarly packed breakfastsTrail and weather briefingsBoot and pole storageLaundryTransfers to El Calafate

Who it is best for

  • Hikers
  • Solo travellers
  • Photographers waiting on weather

Who it is not for

  • Travellers wanting a spa or pool
  • Anyone visiting outside the summer season

Best time to visit

November to March; March for autumn colour and thinner crowds.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Trailhead position
  • Hiker-focused service
  • Mountain views from common areas

Considerations

  • Seasonal — closed much of the winter
  • Basic facilities by design
  • Patagonian wind is constant

Nearby attractions

  • Laguna de los Tres trailhead — from the door
  • Laguna Torre trailhead — 10 min walk
  • Chorrillo del Salto — 20 min walk
  • El Calafate and Perito Moreno — 3 hr drive
