Maasai Mara · Kenya

Olare Ridge Camp

Nine tents in a community conservancy

Overview

A nine-tent camp on a ridge in a Mara conservancy bordering the national reserve, with vehicle numbers capped at sightings and permission for night drives and walking safaris that the reserve does not allow. Rates are all-inclusive of drives, meals and conservancy fees.

Location

A community conservancy on the northern boundary of the Maasai Mara National Reserve.

Why stay here

  • Conservancy rules mean a maximum of a few vehicles at any sighting — the single biggest difference in safari quality.
  • Night drives and walking safaris are permitted here and prohibited in the reserve.
  • Conservancy fees go directly to the Maasai landowning families.
  • Nine tents means the camp never feels like a hotel.

Best rooms

  • Ridge-end tents for the widest plains outlook and the most privacy.
  • The family tent for two adults and two children.
  • Central tents are closer to the mess and easier for late returns.

Amenities

All-inclusive game drivesNight drives and walking safarisMaasai guides from the conservancyBush breakfasts and sundownersLaundryAirstrip transfers

Who it is best for

  • Photographers
  • Repeat safari travellers
  • Anyone prioritising sighting quality over cost

Who it is not for

  • Budget travellers
  • Very young children — check the camp's minimum age

Best time to visit

July to October for the migration; January to March for cats and calving with far fewer people.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Low vehicle density at sightings
  • Activities the reserve does not permit
  • Direct community benefit

Considerations

  • Premium all-inclusive pricing
  • Fly-in transfers add cost
  • Unfenced camp — escorts required at night

Nearby attractions

  • Mara river crossings — 45 min drive in season
  • Maasai Mara National Reserve — adjacent
  • Maasai village visits — arranged with the conservancy
  • Balloon launch site — 20 min drive
