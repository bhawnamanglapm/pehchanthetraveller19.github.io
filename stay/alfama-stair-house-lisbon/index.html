Alfama Stair House

Nine rooms in the oldest quarter

Overview

A narrow nine-room house on an Alfama staircase, with a roof terrace that looks over the tiled roofs to the Tagus. Fado houses are on the same street; tram 28 passes two streets below.

Location

Alfama, Lisbon — 15 minutes' walk from Baixa, straight up.

Why stay here

  • Alfama at night, after the day visitors leave, is the best version of Lisbon.
  • The roof terrace at sunset is the property's real room.
  • Two long-running fado houses within a two-minute walk.
  • Priced well below the neighbourhoods below it.

Best rooms

  • The two river-view rooms on the upper floors.
  • Rear rooms are quieter but face the hill.
  • The ground-floor room is the only one without stairs — and the darkest.

Amenities

Roof terraceBreakfastLuggage transfer to and from the taxi drop pointFado booking assistance

Who it is best for

  • Solo travellers
  • Couples on a budget
  • Travellers who want atmosphere over facilities

Who it is not for

  • Anyone with heavy luggage or mobility limits
  • Light sleepers — this is a lively neighbourhood

Best time to visit

May, June or September; June for the Santo António street festivals.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Best-value location in central Lisbon
  • Genuine neighbourhood setting
  • Outstanding roof terrace

Considerations

  • Steep stairs to reach the door
  • Taxis cannot get close
  • Street noise on festival nights

Nearby attractions

  • Miradouro das Portas do Sol — 4 min walk
  • São Jorge castle — 10 min walk
  • Sé cathedral — 6 min walk
  • Fado museum — 8 min walk
