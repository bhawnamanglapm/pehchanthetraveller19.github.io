Urubamba Valley Hacienda

A restored hacienda at 2,870m

Overview

Twenty-four rooms in a colonial hacienda on the Urubamba river, deliberately positioned 500m lower than Cusco so guests acclimatise while they sleep. Gardens, an oxygen-enriched room for guests who need it, and Ollantaytambo twenty minutes away for the Machu Picchu train.

Location

Urubamba, Sacred Valley — 1 hour from Cusco, 20 minutes from the Ollantaytambo train station.

Why stay here

  • Sleeping at 2,870m rather than Cusco's 3,400m is the best altitude decision available to travellers here.
  • Twenty minutes from the Machu Picchu train, which makes early departures manageable.
  • Working gardens supply the kitchen.
  • The valley is warmer and greener than the city above it.

Best rooms

  • Garden-facing rooms in the original wing.
  • River-side rooms for the sound.
  • The family rooms in the newer wing are larger but less characterful.

Amenities

GardensOxygen-enriched roomSpaTrain station transfersCooking and weaving workshopsBicycle hire

Who it is best for

  • Families
  • Travellers concerned about altitude
  • Anyone using the valley as a Machu Picchu base

Who it is not for

  • Travellers who want to be in Cusco's centre
  • Very short two-night trips

Best time to visit

May to September, the dry season.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Meaningfully lower altitude
  • Close to the Machu Picchu train
  • Genuine hacienda architecture

Considerations

  • An hour from Cusco's restaurants and museums
  • Cool evenings year-round
  • Machu Picchu tickets must be booked separately and early

Nearby attractions

  • Ollantaytambo — 20 min drive
  • Moray and Maras — 40 min drive
  • Chinchero weaving village — 35 min drive
  • Cusco — 60 min drive
