Príncipe Real Townhouse

A tiled nineteenth-century house with a garden

Overview

Sixteen rooms in a restored townhouse in Príncipe Real, with the original azulejo tiling in the stairwell and a walled garden at the back that most guests do not expect. Walkable downhill to Chiado and Bairro Alto, and level with the best independent shops in the city.

Location

Príncipe Real, Lisbon — 10 minutes downhill to Chiado, on the flat for the neighbourhood itself.

Why stay here

  • Príncipe Real is the best-positioned neighbourhood in Lisbon: central, calm, and level rather than vertical.
  • The walled garden is a genuine luxury in a dense city.
  • Original tilework and joinery kept through the restoration.
  • Small enough to get real recommendations at the desk.

Best rooms

  • Garden-facing rooms at the back, which are silent.
  • The two front rooms with wrought-iron balconies over the street.
  • Top-floor rooms have the best light and the most stairs.

Amenities

Walled gardenBreakfast with Portuguese pastry from a local bakerySmall honesty barBicycle storageConcierge for Sintra and fado bookings

Who it is best for

  • Solo travellers
  • Couples
  • Repeat visitors to Lisbon

Who it is not for

  • Families needing multiple connecting rooms
  • Anyone needing a pool or gym

Best time to visit

April to June, or September and October.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Excellent neighbourhood
  • Quiet garden rooms
  • Real character in the building

Considerations

  • Small lift that does not reach every floor
  • Lisbon's hills start at the door
  • Street-facing rooms hear late-night traffic at weekends

Nearby attractions

  • Príncipe Real garden — 3 min walk
  • Chiado — 10 min walk downhill
  • Bairro Alto — 8 min walk
  • Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara — 6 min walk
