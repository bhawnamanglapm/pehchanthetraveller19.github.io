Wadi Rum · Jordan

Rum Star Camp

Bedouin-run camp deep in the protected area

Overview

A family-run camp of twelve goat-hair tents and six domed units, set against a jebel forty minutes inside the protected area. Dinner is zarb cooked in the sand; the evenings are fire, tea and an unusually good silence.

Location

Wadi Rum protected area — 40 minutes by 4x4 from the visitor centre.

Why stay here

  • Being deep inside the protected area means no vehicle noise and no light from the village.
  • Run by a local Bedouin family, so the money stays in the community that manages the land.
  • The domed units let you watch the sky from bed without giving up warmth.
  • Zarb dinner cooked properly, in the ground, every night.

Best rooms

  • The domed units for the sky view, best in the cold months.
  • Traditional tents for the authentic version and better shade in warm months.
  • The two furthest tents for the quietest position.

Amenities

Zarb dinners4x4 desert toursCamel ridesStargazingShared and private bathrooms depending on unitTransfers from the visitor centre

Who it is best for

  • Families
  • Couples
  • First-time desert travellers

Who it is not for

  • Anyone needing constant power and connectivity
  • Travellers uncomfortable with basic facilities

Best time to visit

March to May and September to November.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Deep-desert position
  • Community-owned
  • Excellent value

Considerations

  • Cold nights, including below freezing in winter
  • Limited electricity and no reliable signal
  • Shared facilities in the traditional tents

Nearby attractions

  • Khazali canyon — 20 min by 4x4
  • Um Fruth rock bridge — 25 min by 4x4
  • Lawrence's Spring — 30 min by 4x4
  • Petra — 2 hr drive
