Ubud · Indonesia

Rice Field Bale

Eight bales in the Sidemen valley

Overview

Eight open-fronted bale pavilions built into working rice terraces in the Sidemen valley, an hour east of Ubud. There is a spring-fed pool, one shared kitchen table, and Mount Agung directly ahead on clear mornings.

Location

Sidemen valley, east Bali — 60 minutes from Ubud, 90 from the airport.

Why stay here

  • Sidemen is what Ubud's rice country looked like twenty years ago, and it is still working farmland.
  • The bale design means you sleep effectively outdoors, with the terrace sounds.
  • Agung views on clear mornings are the best in Bali.
  • Small, quiet, and priced far below its setting.

Best rooms

  • The two upper bales for the Agung sightline.
  • Lower bales sit closer to the water channels and are cooler.
  • There are no interior rooms — this is an open-air property throughout.

Amenities

Spring-fed poolShared long-table dinnersRice terrace walks with the farmerWeaving village visitsMotorbike and driver arrangement

Who it is best for

  • Solo travellers
  • Slow travellers
  • Photographers

Who it is not for

  • Anyone uncomfortable with open-air rooms and insects
  • Families with small children
  • Travellers who need reliable fast internet

Best time to visit

April to October, dry season, for clear Agung mornings.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Extraordinary setting for the price
  • Genuine village context
  • Very quiet

Considerations

  • Open-air rooms mean insects and rain sound
  • Remote — an hour from Ubud
  • Limited dining beyond the property

Nearby attractions

  • Sidemen rice terraces — on site
  • Besakih temple — 40 min drive
  • Tirta Gangga water palace — 45 min drive
  • Amed coast — 60 min drive
