The Lantern Ryokan

Riverside ryokan with private onsen suites

Overview

Fourteen suites on the Hozu river at the western edge of Arashiyama, each with a private open-air bath fed by the property's own spring. Dinner is a seasonal kaiseki served in-room; mornings begin with the bamboo grove empty before the day visitors arrive.

Location

Arashiyama, western Kyoto — 25 minutes by train from Kyoto station, on the river below the bamboo grove.

Why stay here

  • Private onsen suites are the single biggest upgrade available in a Japanese stay, and this property has them in every room.
  • Staying in Arashiyama means seeing the bamboo grove at 6am with nobody in it.
  • The kaiseki kitchen changes the menu roughly every two weeks with the season.
  • The river frontage is genuinely quiet — a rarity this close to a major sight.

Best rooms

  • River-facing suites on the lower floor, where the bath sits closest to the water.
  • The corner suite for the largest outdoor bath.
  • Garden-side rooms are quieter but lose the river sound.

Amenities

Private open-air onsen in every suiteIn-room kaiseki dinnerCommunal indoor and outdoor bathsTea ceremony roomAirport transfer arrangementYukata and geta provided

Who it is best for

  • Couples and honeymoons
  • Special occasions
  • Travellers wanting one high-value night in a longer trip

Who it is not for

  • Travellers on a tight budget
  • Those who want to be walking distance from central nightlife

Best time to visit

Late November for maples on the river; February for snow on the bamboo, which is rare and extraordinary.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Private onsen in all rooms
  • Serious kaiseki kitchen
  • Early access to Arashiyama

Considerations

  • Well outside the city centre
  • Dinner times are fixed
  • Tattoo policies apply in communal baths — check when booking

Nearby attractions

  • Arashiyama bamboo grove — 10 min walk
  • Tenryu-ji temple — 12 min walk
  • Monkey park — 15 min walk
  • Hozu river boat launch — 5 min walk
