Marrakech · Morocco

Riad Nine Doors

A restored medina riad with two courtyards

Overview

Nine rooms around two courtyards in the northern medina, with a plunge pool under an orange tree and a roof terrace for breakfast and evening mint tea. The owners restored it over four years using the original zellige and tadelakt trades.

Location

Northern medina, Marrakech — 12 minutes' walk to Jemaa el-Fnaa, through the souks.

Why stay here

  • Riads are the reason to stay inside the walls, and this is a genuine restoration rather than a new build.
  • Two courtyards means the property never feels full.
  • The roof terrace looks at the Atlas on clear winter mornings.
  • The team meets guests at the taxi drop and walks them in — which matters here.

Best rooms

  • The first-floor courtyard rooms with the original carved cedar doors.
  • The roof suite for space and light.
  • Ground-floor rooms are cool in summer and dark in winter.

Amenities

Plunge poolRoof terraceHammam roomCooking classesMedina orientation walkAtlas day-trip arrangement

Who it is best for

  • Couples
  • First visits to Marrakech
  • Travellers who want the medina experience

Who it is not for

  • Anyone with large luggage — the last stretch is on foot
  • Travellers wanting a large pool

Best time to visit

March to May, October to November. Winter for Atlas views.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Careful, genuine restoration
  • Excellent value
  • Real help navigating the medina

Considerations

  • Vehicle access ends several minutes away
  • Small plunge pool only
  • Medina sound carries — including the call to prayer

Nearby attractions

  • Jemaa el-Fnaa — 12 min walk
  • Ben Youssef Madrasa — 6 min walk
  • Souks — from the door
  • Le Jardin Secret — 8 min walk
