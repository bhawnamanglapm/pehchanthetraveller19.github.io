Glenorchy Lake Cabins

Six cabins at the head of Lake Wakatipu

Overview

Six timber cabins on the lake shore at Glenorchy, forty-five minutes from Queenstown along one of the best short drives anywhere. Each has a wood burner, a lake-facing deck and no television, which is the point.

Location

Glenorchy — 45 minutes from Queenstown along the Lake Wakatipu road.

Why stay here

  • Glenorchy is where the crowds stop and the Southern Alps begin properly.
  • Six cabins on a lake shore, with the Humboldt range across the water.
  • Routeburn and Rees–Dart trailheads are close.
  • Night skies here are far darker than anywhere near Queenstown.

Best rooms

  • The three lake-edge cabins.
  • The larger cabin at the northern end for families.
  • Set-back cabins are cheaper and sheltered from the wind.

Amenities

Wood burnersLake-facing decksKitchens in every cabinKayaksTrailhead transfersDark-sky viewing area

Who it is best for

  • Couples
  • Walkers using the Routeburn
  • Travellers wanting genuine quiet

Who it is not for

  • Anyone who wants restaurants and nightlife nearby
  • Travellers without a car

Best time to visit

December to March for the Great Walks; April and May for autumn colour.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Exceptional lakeside setting
  • Real seclusion
  • Great Walk trailhead access

Considerations

  • 45 minutes from Queenstown's services
  • Limited dining in Glenorchy
  • Sandflies in summer

Nearby attractions

  • Routeburn Track trailhead — 25 min drive
  • Glenorchy lagoon walkway — 5 min walk
  • Paradise valley — 20 min drive
  • Queenstown — 45 min drive
