Munnar · Kerala

High Range Tea Bungalow

A planter's bungalow on a working estate

Overview

A six-room colonial-era planter's bungalow on a working tea estate above Munnar, with fireplaces in every room, a verandah facing the high ranges, and estate walks led by the plantation's own staff at first light.

Location

A working tea estate above Munnar, Kerala — 25 minutes from Munnar town, at around 1,700m.

Why stay here

  • The estate bungalow is the definitive Munnar stay and there are very few genuine ones.
  • Six rooms on a large estate means real solitude.
  • Walks are led by people who work the plantation, not by hotel guides.
  • The verandah at 6am, with the mist below, is the reason to come.

Best rooms

  • The two front rooms opening onto the verandah.
  • The corner room for the widest range view.
  • Rear rooms are warmer in winter and face the garden.

Amenities

Estate walks with plantation staffFireplaces in all roomsFull-board diningTea tastingJeep to KolukkumalaiLibrary

Who it is best for

  • Couples
  • Slow travellers
  • Anyone wanting the estate experience rather than a hotel

Who it is not for

  • Travellers wanting a pool and spa
  • Anyone who needs to be in Munnar town

Best time to visit

September to March; December and January for the clearest mornings.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • A genuine working estate
  • Very few rooms
  • Exceptional early-morning views

Considerations

  • 25 minutes on winding roads from town
  • Cold nights — fireplaces are needed
  • Monsoon landslides can affect access

Nearby attractions

  • Eravikulam National Park — 30 min drive
  • Kolukkumalai tea estate — 90 min by jeep
  • Top Station — 45 min drive
  • Munnar town — 25 min drive
