Hoi An · Vietnam

River Lantern House

Twelve rooms between the old town and the rice fields

Overview

A small colonial-style house on the quiet side of the Thu Bon river, twelve rooms around a pool and a mango tree. Bicycles are included and are the point: the old town is eight minutes one way, the rice fields and An Bang beach twenty minutes the other.

Location

Cam Chau, across the river from Hoi An old town — 8 minutes by bicycle to the lanterns, 20 to the beach.

Why stay here

  • Being just outside the old town means you sleep in silence and still walk in for dinner.
  • The bicycle-and-rice-field position is the best of both Hoi An experiences.
  • Small enough that staff learn what you drink at breakfast on day two.
  • Exceptional value for the standard of room.

Best rooms

  • Upper-floor pool-view rooms with balconies.
  • The garden suite for space if you are staying a week.
  • Ground-floor rooms are darker but cooler in summer.

Amenities

PoolFree bicyclesBeach shuttleCooking class arrangementLaundryTailor recommendations and transfers

Who it is best for

  • Solo travellers
  • Couples
  • Slow travellers staying a week or more

Who it is not for

  • Travellers who want to step straight out into the old town
  • Anyone who cannot or will not cycle

Best time to visit

February to April. Avoid October and November, when this side of the river can flood.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Very good value
  • Quiet, with easy access both ways
  • Genuinely helpful small team

Considerations

  • Cycling is essential to the experience
  • Flood risk in late autumn
  • No lift

Nearby attractions

  • Hoi An old town — 8 min cycle
  • An Bang beach — 20 min cycle
  • Tra Que herb village — 15 min cycle
  • Cam Thanh coconut palms — 10 min cycle
