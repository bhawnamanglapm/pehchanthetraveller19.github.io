Banff & Lake Louise · Canada

Canmore Larch Lodge

A timber lodge outside the park boundary

Overview

Thirty rooms and eight apartment suites in Canmore, twenty minutes outside the Banff park gate, with kitchens in the suites and a ski and bike workshop in the basement. Better value than in-park properties and closer to the trailheads at the Bow valley's eastern end.

Location

Canmore, Alberta — 20 minutes from Banff townsite, outside the national park boundary.

Why stay here

  • Park accommodation is capped by legislation, which makes in-park rates high; Canmore is consistently better value.
  • Apartment suites with kitchens matter on a week-long mountain trip.
  • Canmore has a working-town feel that Banff has largely traded away.
  • Quick access to both the park and the Kananaskis trails.

Best rooms

  • Three Sisters-facing suites with balconies.
  • The two-bedroom apartments for families.
  • Standard rooms at the rear face the road.

Amenities

Kitchen suitesSki and bike workshopHot tubTrail advice deskBear spray rentalPark shuttle information

Who it is best for

  • Families
  • Week-long mountain trips
  • Travellers self-catering

Who it is not for

  • Anyone wanting to walk to Banff Avenue
  • Travellers without a car who need in-park proximity

Best time to visit

July to September for hiking; late September for larch season; December to March for skiing.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Strong value outside the park
  • Genuine kitchens
  • Excellent trail access both directions

Considerations

  • A car is effectively required
  • Park pass needed separately
  • 20 minutes from Banff attractions

Nearby attractions

  • Grassi Lakes trail — 10 min drive
  • Banff townsite — 20 min drive
  • Lake Louise — 60 min drive
  • Kananaskis Country — 25 min drive
