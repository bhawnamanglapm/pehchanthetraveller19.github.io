Old City Teak House

A teak Lanna house inside the moat

Overview

Ten rooms in a restored northern-Thai teak house within the old city walls, arranged around a courtyard with a fig tree and a small pool. Breakfast is northern Thai rather than continental, and the front desk arranges the ethical elephant sanctuaries rather than the others.

Location

Inside the old city moat, Chiang Mai — walking distance to Wat Chedi Luang and the Sunday walking street.

Why stay here

  • Traditional Lanna teak construction is disappearing in Chiang Mai; this is a genuine one.
  • Everything inside the moat is walkable from the door.
  • The team is unusually well informed about which operators to trust.
  • Excellent value for a boutique property in this location.

Best rooms

  • Upstairs teak rooms with the original shutters.
  • The courtyard room for direct pool access.
  • Street-side rooms hear the early monks' alms round — a feature or a problem, depending on you.

Amenities

Courtyard poolNorthern Thai breakfastBicyclesCooking class arrangementAirport transferLaundry

Who it is best for

  • Solo travellers
  • First visits to Chiang Mai
  • Travellers who want to walk everywhere

Who it is not for

  • Anyone needing a gym or business facilities
  • Light sleepers sensitive to early-morning street sound

Best time to visit

November to February. Avoid March and April for air quality.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Authentic architecture
  • Unbeatable walkable location
  • Reliable, ethical activity advice

Considerations

  • Wooden building carries sound
  • Small pool
  • Steep teak stairs, no lift

Nearby attractions

  • Wat Chedi Luang — 5 min walk
  • Sunday walking street — on the doorstep
  • Three Kings Monument — 7 min walk
  • Doi Suthep — 30 min drive
