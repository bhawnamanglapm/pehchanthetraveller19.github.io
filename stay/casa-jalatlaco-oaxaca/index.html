Casa Jalatlaco

Eleven rooms in a colonial courtyard house

Overview

A restored colonial house in Jalatlaco, eleven rooms around a courtyard planted with citrus, ten minutes' walk from the Zócalo through the neighbourhood's painted streets. Breakfast is regional, and the kitchen runs a market-to-table cooking class twice a week.

Location

Jalatlaco, Oaxaca de Juárez — 10 minutes' walk from the historic centre.

Why stay here

  • Jalatlaco is the best neighbourhood in the city: quiet, walkable, and full of small restaurants.
  • Courtyard houses are the local architectural form and this one is intact.
  • The cooking class starts at the market, which is the right way to do it.
  • Very good value for the standard of restoration.

Best rooms

  • Upper courtyard rooms with the original beamed ceilings.
  • The corner room for street light and neighbourhood sound.
  • Interior rooms are quiet and darker.

Amenities

CourtyardRegional breakfastMarket cooking classesMezcal tastingsValley tour arrangementRoof terrace

Who it is best for

  • Solo travellers
  • Food-led trips
  • Couples

Who it is not for

  • Travellers wanting a pool or resort facilities
  • Anyone who needs to be on the Zócalo itself

Best time to visit

October to April. Book many months ahead for Día de Muertos.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Outstanding neighbourhood
  • Serious food programme
  • Excellent value

Considerations

  • No pool
  • Cobbled streets throughout the neighbourhood
  • Fills completely around Día de Muertos

Nearby attractions

  • Santo Domingo — 8 min walk
  • Mercado 20 de Noviembre — 12 min walk
  • Monte Albán — 30 min drive
  • Teotitlán del Valle — 45 min drive
