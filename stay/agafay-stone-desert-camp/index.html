Agafay Stone Desert Camp

Tented suites an hour from the medina

Overview

Eighteen tented suites in the Agafay stone desert, with a pool facing the Atlas and no other lights in sight. Not sand dunes — a lunar landscape of grey hills — and forty minutes from Marrakech rather than ten hours from it.

Location

Agafay desert — 40 minutes southwest of Marrakech, with the Atlas on the horizon.

Why stay here

  • Desert silence and Atlas views without the two-day drive to Merzouga.
  • The stone desert is visually distinct from sand and photographs beautifully at dusk.
  • Pairs naturally with two or three medina nights.
  • Genuinely dark skies this close to a major city.

Best rooms

  • Atlas-facing suites on the ridge line.
  • Pool suites for privacy.
  • Lower suites are more sheltered from wind.

Amenities

Pool with Atlas viewsTented suites with private bathroomsCamel and quad excursionsStargazingMedina shuttleFull-board dining

Who it is best for

  • Couples
  • Short desert add-ons to a Marrakech trip
  • Photographers

Who it is not for

  • Travellers expecting sand dunes
  • Anyone visiting in the July–August heat

Best time to visit

March to May and October to November; winter nights are cold but clear.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Close to Marrakech
  • Real desert quiet
  • Strong Atlas views

Considerations

  • Stone, not sand — set expectations
  • Transfers required for everything
  • Wind exposure on the ridge

Nearby attractions

  • Marrakech medina — 40 min drive
  • Lalla Takerkoust lake — 25 min drive
  • Atlas foothills — 40 min drive
  • Ourika valley — 60 min drive
