Udaipur · Rajasthan

Pichola Haveli

A lakeside haveli with a rooftop restaurant

Overview

Fourteen rooms in a restored family haveli on the eastern shore of Lake Pichola, with a rooftop restaurant facing the City Palace and the Aravalli ridge behind it. Rooms keep the original jharokha windows and the frescoed alcoves.

Location

Lal Ghat, Udaipur — on Lake Pichola, 5 minutes' walk from the City Palace entrance.

Why stay here

  • Lake-facing haveli rooms are the reason to stay in Udaipur rather than a modern hotel outside the old city.
  • The rooftop faces the palace, and dinner there at dusk is the city's defining experience.
  • Five minutes' walk to the City Palace gate and the boat jetty.
  • Family-run, with the restoration done room by room over a decade.

Best rooms

  • Lake-facing rooms on the second and third floors with jharokha windows.
  • The frescoed corner suite.
  • Interior rooms are cool in summer and considerably cheaper.

Amenities

Rooftop restaurantLake viewsBoat trip arrangementCooking classesMiniature painting demonstrationsAirport transfers

Who it is best for

  • Couples
  • First visits to Udaipur
  • Photographers

Who it is not for

  • Travellers needing a pool or lift
  • Anyone sensitive to early-morning temple sound

Best time to visit

October to March; September for full lakes after the monsoon.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Genuine lakeside haveli
  • Outstanding rooftop outlook
  • Walking distance to everything

Considerations

  • Steep original stairs, no lift
  • Old city vehicle access is limited
  • Ghat area is busy during festivals

Nearby attractions

  • City Palace — 5 min walk
  • Jagdish Temple — 3 min walk
  • Bagore ki Haveli — 4 min walk
  • Lake Pichola jetty — 2 min walk
