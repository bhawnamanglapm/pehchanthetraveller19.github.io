An Bang Sand House

Beachfront rooms and villas on An Bang

Overview

Twenty rooms and four villas directly on An Bang beach, built low and wide with sea-facing terraces. The beach here is public and used by locals in the late afternoon, which keeps it feeling like a place rather than a resort strip.

Location

An Bang beach, 4km from Hoi An old town — regular shuttle, or 20 minutes by bicycle.

Why stay here

  • Direct beach access on the best stretch of coast near Hoi An.
  • The villas have private plunge pools and work well for families or two couples.
  • Sunrise from the terrace is the reason to book the sea-facing rooms.
  • Genuinely good beachfront restaurants next door, not just the hotel's own.

Best rooms

  • Sea-facing terrace rooms on the first floor.
  • The two-bedroom villas for families.
  • Garden rooms are cheaper and quieter but lose the sea entirely.

Amenities

Direct beach accessTwo poolsBicyclesOld town shuttleKids' poolSurfboard and kayak hire in season

Who it is best for

  • Families
  • Couples wanting beach time
  • Longer stays combining beach and town

Who it is not for

  • Travellers who want to be in the old town
  • Anyone visiting during the winter swell, when swimming is limited

Best time to visit

May to August for calm sea and reliable sun.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • True beachfront
  • Good family configuration
  • Strong local restaurant scene alongside

Considerations

  • Winter swell can make swimming unsafe
  • Old town requires the shuttle or a cycle
  • Beach is public and busy at weekends

Nearby attractions

  • An Bang beach — on site
  • Hoi An old town — 15 min drive
  • Cham Islands boat trips — seasonal
  • Da Nang and Marble Mountains — 35 min drive
