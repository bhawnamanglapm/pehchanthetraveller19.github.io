Mae Rim Forest Lodge

Hill villas above the coffee valleys

Overview

Sixteen villas stepped down a forested hillside forty minutes north of Chiang Mai, above the Mae Rim coffee valleys. There is a spa built around northern Thai massage traditions, a long infinity pool facing the ridge, and a lot of deliberate quiet.

Location

Mae Rim district — 40 minutes from Chiang Mai old city, in the hills above the Samoeng loop.

Why stay here

  • The temperature drops noticeably at this elevation — the difference is real in warm months.
  • The Samoeng loop and coffee farms start at the gate.
  • The spa is a serious one rather than a hotel add-on.
  • Villa privacy without resort scale.

Best rooms

  • Ridge-facing villas at the top of the property for the view.
  • Lower villas sit closer to the stream and are cooler.
  • The two pool villas for longer stays.

Amenities

Infinity poolNorthern Thai spaCoffee farm walksShuttle to the old cityYoga pavilionRestaurant sourcing from the valley

Who it is best for

  • Couples
  • Wellness-led trips
  • Travellers combining city and hills

Who it is not for

  • Anyone who wants to walk to restaurants and nightlife
  • Short two-night city visits

Best time to visit

November to February for cool, clear mornings.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Genuine hill setting and cooler air
  • Strong spa programme
  • Easy access to the Samoeng loop

Considerations

  • Requires the shuttle or a car for the city
  • Steep paths between villas
  • Limited dining options nearby

Nearby attractions

  • Mae Rim coffee farms — 10 min drive
  • Samoeng loop — from the gate
  • Mae Sa waterfall — 20 min drive
  • Chiang Mai old city — 40 min drive
