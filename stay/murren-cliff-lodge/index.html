Lauterbrunnen Valley · Switzerland

Mürren Cliff Lodge

Car-free village lodge facing the Eiger

Overview

A twenty-two room timber lodge in Mürren, reachable only by cable car and mountain railway, looking directly across the valley at the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau. Half-board is the norm here because there are few alternatives, and the kitchen is better than it needs to be.

Location

Mürren, 1,650m — cable car from Lauterbrunnen or Stechelberg; no road access.

Why stay here

  • The three-peak view from the balconies is the reason people come to this valley.
  • Car-free means the village is genuinely silent after the last cable car.
  • The Schilthorn cable car and the Mürren–Gimmelwald path both start here.
  • Snow-sure in winter and trail-connected in summer.

Best rooms

  • South-facing balcony rooms for the full Eiger line-up.
  • Family rooms under the eaves.
  • North-facing rooms are cheaper and look at the cliff instead.

Amenities

Half-board diningDrying room and ski storageSaunaTrail and avalanche informationLuggage transfer from the valley station

Who it is best for

  • Walkers and skiers
  • Families
  • Travellers who want mountain quiet

Who it is not for

  • Anyone arriving late at night — cable cars stop
  • Travellers who want a car at the door

Best time to visit

Late June to September for trails; January to March for skiing.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Unobstructed three-peak view
  • Car-free silence
  • Direct trail and lift access

Considerations

  • Cable car times govern your day
  • Swiss pricing
  • High wind can close the lifts

Nearby attractions

  • Schilthorn cable car — 5 min walk
  • Gimmelwald path — from the door
  • Lauterbrunnen valley floor — 15 min by cable car
  • Jungfraujoch railway — 40 min via Lauterbrunnen
