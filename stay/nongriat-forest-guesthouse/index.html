Nongriat Forest Guesthouse

Simple rooms beside the root bridges

Overview

Six simple rooms in Nongriat village, reachable only on foot down roughly three thousand steps, beside the double-decker living root bridge. There is no road, no vehicle noise, and the river pools are a two-minute walk away.

Location

Nongriat village, East Khasi Hills — a 3,000-step descent from Tyrna, no road access.

Why stay here

  • Staying overnight means having the root bridges to yourself in the early morning and evening.
  • The descent and ascent are far more enjoyable split across two days.
  • The river pools below the village are extraordinary and almost empty at dawn.
  • Village-run, with the money staying in Nongriat.

Best rooms

  • The two rooms facing the forest.
  • All rooms are simple — expect a bed, a fan and a shared or basic private bathroom.

Amenities

Home-cooked Khasi mealsGuided walks to Rainbow FallsRoot bridge accessRiver pool swimmingPorter arrangement for luggage

Who it is best for

  • Trekkers
  • Solo travellers
  • Anyone prioritising the experience over comfort

Who it is not for

  • Travellers with mobility limitations — there is no road
  • Anyone needing air conditioning or reliable power

Best time to visit

October to April. Steps are dangerously slippery in monsoon.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • Root bridges at dawn with nobody there
  • Genuine village stay
  • Very inexpensive

Considerations

  • 3,000 steps each way, carrying your own bag or hiring a porter
  • Basic facilities and intermittent electricity
  • Leeches and slippery steps in the wet months

Nearby attractions

  • Double-decker root bridge — 5 min walk
  • Rainbow Falls — 90 min walk
  • Natural river pools — 10 min walk
  • Cherrapunji — 1 hr drive from Tyrna
