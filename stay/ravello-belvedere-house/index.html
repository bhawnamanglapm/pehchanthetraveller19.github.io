Ravello Belvedere House

A cliff-top villa 350 metres above the sea

Overview

Twelve rooms in a converted villa on the Ravello ridge, with gardens running to the cliff edge and the whole Gulf of Salerno below. Ravello sits high above the coast road, which is why it is quiet when everything beneath it is not.

Location

Ravello — 350m above the coast, 25 minutes by road from Amalfi.

Why stay here

  • Ravello is the only town on this coast that is genuinely calm in high season.
  • The elevation gives you the entire gulf rather than one bay.
  • Gardens and a cliff-edge pool rather than a beach — a fair trade at this height.
  • The summer music festival is a short walk away.

Best rooms

  • Gulf-facing rooms on the upper floor.
  • The garden suite for the terrace.
  • Interior rooms are cooler in August but have no view.

Amenities

Cliff-edge poolGardensSpa treatment roomsBeach club shuttlePrivate boat arrangementConcierge for restaurant bookings

Who it is best for

  • Special occasions
  • Couples
  • Travellers prioritising quiet over beach access

Who it is not for

  • Anyone who wants to walk to the sea
  • Budget-conscious trips

Best time to visit

June for the music festival; late September for warm sea and thinner crowds.

Pros and considerations

Pros

  • The best views on the coast
  • Genuinely quiet in season
  • Serious gardens

Considerations

  • Far above the beaches — every swim needs a transfer
  • Premium pricing
  • Winding access road

Nearby attractions

  • Villa Cimbrone — 10 min walk
  • Villa Rufolo — 8 min walk
  • Amalfi — 25 min drive
  • Path of the Gods — 30 min drive
