# Positioning and build order, optimised for collaborations

The founder's goal is collaborations — hosted stays, sponsored content, brand and
tourism-board partnerships — across both Indian and international travel. Every
decision below is judged on one question: **does this make a partner say yes?**

---

## What a partner actually evaluates

1. **Reach and engagement** — the numbers. The site cannot manufacture these.
2. **Audience fit** — "do this creator's followers book places like mine?"
3. **Proof of work** — what would we actually get for our money.
4. **Professionalism** — does this person look like a business.

The website moves 2, 3 and 4. It cannot move 1. So the site's job is to make a
specific, credible claim about *who this audience is* and *what a partner
receives* — and to make enquiring frictionless.

## The positioning that wins the most yeses

**Specialist in spiritual and Himalayan India, with genuine Asia + Gulf range.**

Not "a global travel site". A generalist creator competes with every travel
account on earth on follower count alone — a fight decided by numbers, not by
merit. A specialist competes on *audience ownership*, which is what a partner
is actually buying.

Concretely, this is the difference between two pitches:

> "I'm a travel creator with X followers."

> "I reach travellers who plan Char Dham, Ayodhya and Vaishno Devi trips —
> the exact people booking rooms in your city this season."

The second wins with a hotel in Ayodhya, a Katra property, or Uttarakhand
Tourism. The first is a numbers auction.

The international footprint — Dubai, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Nepal — is
kept and used, because it proves range and because those are the most
collaboration-rich destinations on the list. It is the *supporting* claim, not
the headline.

## Build order, ranked by collaboration yield

Not by how interesting the place is. By how likely a partner is to respond.

### Tier 1 — build first
| Destination | Why it converts |
|---|---|
| **Ayodhya** | Large new hotel supply with no content history and urgent need for it; enormous search growth; UP Tourism actively promoting. Highest yield on the list. |
| **Jim Corbett** | Dense resort market that already hosts creators routinely. Established collaboration norms. |
| **Goa** | Deepest boutique and resort market in India for hosted stays. |
| **Dubai** | Among the most creator-friendly destinations anywhere; active tourism-board and hotel programmes. |
| **Bir Billing** | Distinctive, adventure operators and homestays, low competition for well-made English content. |

### Tier 2
Vaishno Devi (Katra) · Ujjain · Vrindavan · Shimla · Thailand · Vietnam

### Tier 3
Kedarnath · Badrinath · Gangotri · Uttarkashi · Barsana · Khatu Shyam Ji ·
Salasar Balaji · Amritsar · Malaysia · Nepal

Tier 3 is not lower value — Char Dham is the strongest *audience* content on the
list and should be built for authority and search. It is simply slower to convert
into a hosted stay, because the accommodation market there is temple trusts,
dharamshalas and small operators rather than properties with marketing budgets.

**Build Tier 1 for revenue. Build Tier 3 for the audience that makes Tier 1
partners care.**

### Cities — deprioritised
Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, Chandigarh. Real experience, but the most
saturated creator market in the country. Weakest return per guide written.

## The missing piece: a media kit

The `/partner/` page currently explains services honestly and declines to quote
audience numbers. That is the right posture for a site with nothing to show —
and the wrong one for active outreach.

For collaborations it needs:

- **Audience**: follower count, engagement rate, reach, audience geography and
  age split — real figures from Instagram Insights, no estimates.
- **What a partner receives**: deliverables per package, stated plainly.
- **Proof of work**: real destination guides and real photography.
- **A downloadable one-page kit** for email pitches.

This requires the founder's actual numbers. Nothing here will be estimated or
filled with a plausible figure.

## Sequence

1. Founder supplies Instagram numbers → build the media kit.
2. Founder is interviewed on Tier 1 destinations, one at a time.
3. Each guide replaces an invented one; the generic set is deleted as it is
   displaced.
4. Outreach begins with the pitch: *"here is my guide to your destination."*
