## 1. Meta app review (Instagram API)

**Answer**
- There are two ways in. **Instagram API with Instagram Login** "serves Instagram professional accounts without
  requiring a linked Facebook Page". The Facebook Login version needs a linked Facebook Page. Both serve only
  **professional accounts** (business or creator).
- **Permissions for insights** with Instagram Login: `instagram_business_basic` and
  `instagram_business_manage_insights`.
- **Standard Access** is the default. It covers app users "with roles on your app", or testing. So the owner's own
  account can be connected and tested **without review**.
- **Advanced Access** is needed to serve accounts we don't own, which means every traveller. It "requires App Review
  and Business Verification".
- **What review needs:**
  - a description of how each permission is used;
  - **one screencast per permission**, showing the feature end to end;
  - test login steps and credentials;
  - an app icon, a privacy policy URL, a category and a business email.
- **Review can't happen before the feature exists.** "To request Advanced Access to certain permissions, you need to
  make at least 1 successful API call." The reviewer must also be able to test the app.
- **Business Verification** is needed for apps that request Advanced Access. Only an Admin of the Meta business
  portfolio can do it. The developer page doesn't list the documents; it points to Meta's Business Help Centre.
- **Limits worth knowing:**
  - "Some metrics are not available on Instagram accounts with fewer than 100 followers."
  - Account metrics are kept by Meta for up to 90 days, so older numbers must come from our own stored copies.

**Not found:** how long review takes. The page gives no time.

**Verdict: Owner step now; submit with 3.07.** The 3.01 acceptance box "Meta app review submitted" **can't be ticked
in 3.01**: Meta only accepts a submission once the connect flow works. The slow parts can start now (see "What the
owner needs to do"). The review is submitted as part of 3.07.

## 2. Storing YouTube numbers past 30 days

**Answer.** The answer depends on how the numbers are read.

| How the numbers are read | Rule | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| **With the creator's own sign-in** (OAuth: YouTube Analytics API, or view counts read with their token) = "Authorized Data" | May be stored "for as long as is necessary". The app "must still ensure every 30 days" "that it is still authorized by the user to access that data" (III.E.4.b) | 3.07, 3.08 |
| **Without sign-in** (public channel numbers) = "Non-Authorized Data" | Never stored more than 30 days; delete or refresh (III.E.4.b, III.E.4.d) | 2.02 (unchanged) |

Other rules that apply to 3.07 and 3.08:
- **Disconnecting:** when a user revokes access through our own disconnect button, all their Authorized Data is
  deleted "within 7 calendar days". If they revoke it in Google's security settings instead, the limit is 30 days
  (III.D.2.3).
- **Deletion requests:** users must be able to ask us to delete their stored data, and it must go "within 7 calendar
  days" (III.E.4.g). This fits 3.04.
- **Old numbers may be shown** if they are "presented accurately in context of time" (III.E.4.f). In practice, every
  number carries its date.
- **No home-made metrics.** Apps "must not … access or use API Data to create new or derived data or metrics"
  (III.E.4.h). For example, an engagement rate or growth % worked out from YouTube numbers.
  - The **Derived Metrics policy** allows this, and storage of statistics for up to 36 calendar months, only for
    "accepted API Clients". Those are apps whose analytics use case Google has accepted through its form.
  - Until then, partner reports show YouTube's numbers as returned, each with its date.

**Verdict: Build (₹0).** 3.07 doesn't need the 30-day delete rule for connected numbers. It needs:
- a check every 30 days that the creator's authorisation is still valid; if it isn't, the stored numbers are deleted;
- deletion within 7 days of a disconnect or a deletion request;
- a date on every number;
- no derived metrics unless Google accepts a derived-metrics application (optional and free; decide in 3.08).

**Sources**
- [YouTube API Services Developer Policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies) (III.D.2,
  III.E.4, definitions in IV)
- [Derived Metrics policy](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/derived-metrics-policy)

**Options compared**

| | Supabase Free | Neon Free | Postgres on the existing Strapi server |
|---|---|---|---|

**Answer**
- **Supabase Free in Mumbai** fits best:
  - it meets 3.02's "in or near India" need;
  - it brings sign-in (3.03) and file storage (screenshots and evidence) in one place.
- **The gaps can be closed at ₹0:**
  - **Backups:** a nightly `pg_dump` to the existing S3 backup bucket, plus one restore test (already a 3.02 acceptance

  - **The weekly pause** only matters before real traffic.
- **Neon** is the fallback if Supabase doesn't suit. It has no India region.
- **The Strapi server** is the ₹0 fallback. It keeps traveller data out of Strapi's own database, but puts both on one

**Still open:**

  already listed in 3.02.
- Supabase's free-plan limits change from time to time. Re-check on the day 3.02 starts.

**Verdict: Build (₹0): Supabase Free, Mumbai, with our own nightly backup.** The owner signs the decision note in 3.02.
Moving to Pro (≈ ₹2,337/month) is the owner's call, when traffic or backup needs justify it.

## 4. Live weather forecast

**Answer**
- **Open-Meteo's free API: not allowed for us.**
  - "You may only use the free API services for non-commercial purposes."
  - Sites "with subscriptions or ads" are listed as commercial. This page earns through affiliate links and, from 3.11,
    a paid tier.
  - Its paid plans grant "a commercial use licence" (Standard: 1M calls/month), but the pricing page shows no price.
- **MET Norway (Norwegian Meteorological Institute): allowed.**
  - Data is under NLOD 2.0 and **CC BY 4.0**. Both allow commercial use with credit, such as «Data from MET Norway».
  - Rules:
    - send a User-Agent that names the app, with a website or email;
    - stay under **20 requests per second** in total;
    - never re-request before the `Expires` time;
    - use `If-Modified-Since`.
  - Break the rules and "you risk being blocked without warning".
  - There's no SLA.
  - The terms page asks for a User-Agent only; it mentions no key or fee.
- **Self-hosting Open-Meteo** is possible (it's open source, and its data is CC BY 4.0), but it needs its own server
  and storage. Not checked further.

**Verdict: Build, limited (₹0)** with MET Norway, if a stage 2 forecast is wanted:
- fetch on the server, cached until `Expires`;
- credit "Data from MET Norway" next to the forecast;
- show a fallback message if the service is down.

"Best months" stays admin-entered, as the plan already says.

**Sources**
- [Open-Meteo terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)
- [Open-Meteo pricing](https://open-meteo.com/en/pricing)
- [MET Norway API terms of service](https://api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService)

## 5. Embedded maps

**Answer**
- **openstreetmap.org's own tile servers: not for us.**
  - "Commercial services, or those that seek donations, should be especially aware that access may be withdrawn at any
    point."
  - "Availability is best-effort: there is no SLA or guarantee."
  - "Access may be blocked without prior notice."
  - Using them also requires:
    - visible attribution;
    - an app-specific User-Agent;
    - a valid Referer;
    - caching each tile for at least 7 days;
    - no bulk or offline downloads.
- **OpenFreeMap:** a public instance that is "completely free".
  - "There are no limits on the number of map views or requests."
  - Commercial use: "Yes".
  - No registration, API key or cookies.
  - Attribution to OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap is required. The MapLibre map library adds it
    automatically.
  - There's no SLA, and self-hosting is possible.
- **Place lookup (Nominatim):** the README rules are unchanged:
  - 1 request per second;
  - cache results;
  - no search-as-you-type;
  - admin looks up each stay's position once.

**Verdict:**
- **Don't build** on openstreetmap.org tiles.
- **Build, limited (₹0)** with OpenFreeMap + MapLibre if 0b or stage 2 wants an embedded map, with attribution
  showing. Keep the "open in a map app" link as the fallback if the tiles don't load.

**Sources**
- [OSM tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/)
- [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/)
- [Nominatim usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/)

## 6. Razorpay for the Creator tier (3.11) and the film pre-sell (3.12)

**What exists already:** `/trip-plan/premium` uses Razorpay's **Orders API**, Standard Checkout, signature checks and a
webhook (`payment.captured`, `payment.failed`, refunds). Orders are saved through a Strapi orders token, and an unlock
cookie is set. Test keys are used until the going-live checklist is done.

**Answer**
- **Fees** (Razorpay's public rate card):
  - 2% per successful card, UPI or netbanking payment, plus 18% GST on the fee;
  - no setup fee, yearly charge or refund fee.
- **Recurring payments (Razorpay Subscriptions):**
  - Plans, then Subscriptions, charged automatically each period.
  - Methods: cards (tokenised), UPI AutoPay and eMandate.
  - **Card subscriptions cost 0.9% plus the 2% platform fee per charge.** UPI and eMandate subscription pricing is "on
    request".
  - Needs Flash Checkout enabled in the Dashboard.
  - Webhook events:
    - `subscription.authenticated`, `activated`, `charged`, `pending`;
    - `halted`, `cancelled`, `completed`;
    - `paused`, `resumed`, `updated`.
- **Payment Pages** (no code): 0.2% plus the gateway fee.

**Answer by task**

| Task | What fits | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **3.12** film pre-sell (one-off) | **The existing Orders pattern** | Already built and tested; 2% fee; nothing new to approve |
| **3.11** Creator tier, first version | **A one-off "period pass" (for example 30 days) on the Orders pattern** | No mandate setup and the lower fee. Cancelling is simple: it just isn't renewed |
| **3.11** later, if travellers ask for auto-renewal | **Razorpay Subscriptions** | Handles renewals itself, but costs more on cards and needs its own webhook events |

**Build notes:**
- The premium price check (₹199–499) is specific to the premium plan. The tier needs its own price setting (from
  2.11).
- Once 3.02 exists, tier and payment records belong in the **traveller database**, not in Strapi.

**Verdict: Build (₹0 to set up; fees are per payment).** The terms and refund wording still need the lawyer before the
first real payment, as 3.11 already says.
