## What it is

One shared trip both people edit, with one Trip Balance split between them. It needs accounts, so it can't come
earlier.

## To design when it starts

- Inviting a friend by email link; both can edit; who owns the trip.
- How spending, earnings and free nights split (equal by default, or per entry).
- What each person sees in their own media kit and partner reports.
- Leaving a shared trip: each person keeps a copy.
