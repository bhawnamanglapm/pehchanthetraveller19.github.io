## Why it matters

Stays that host creators get content and bookings. Once a destination has enough of them, the listing itself can
earn.

## Steps

1. The first hotels list free, to build supply. They start with the 10–20 stays the owner checks after launch (4.06),
   and hotels are approached with "featured in the documentary and on the page" once there's footage from the test
   trip to show (README §11).
2. When a destination reaches about 20 checked stays, test two models with real hotels **before any price is set**:
   a listing fee, or a fee per confirmed collaboration.
3. Pitch with the anonymous totals (2.10) and the worked example.
4. Record each hotel's answer and the result of the test. The owner chooses the model.

## Rules

- **Never tied to the Pehchan-checked badge.** A stay can pay for a creator listing and still fail the check.
- The creator badge always needs the stay's written OK.
- Pehchan doesn't hold money between hotels and creators or guarantee deals.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Test results with real hotels are written down before any price is published.
- [ ] Strapi still refuses Pehchan-checked unless the check passes, whatever the payment status.
