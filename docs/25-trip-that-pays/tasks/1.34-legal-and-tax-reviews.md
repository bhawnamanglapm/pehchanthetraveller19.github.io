## Why it matters

The page tells travellers how to take free stays, label ads, film people and handle money. Wrong wording here is a
legal and trust risk, so nothing goes live unchecked. The system enforces this (1.07): the consent form can't be
published without the lawyer date, and tax wording can't show without the CA date.

**Changed 16 Nov 2026 (owner):** the CA review moved to the people steps at the end of the plan. The pages launch
without the tax line, which stays hidden until the CA date is entered. The lawyer review still comes before launch.

## CA review

- The "free stays can be taxable" wording. Under the old Section 194R, a business giving benefits worth over ₹20,000 a
  year deducts 10% tax at source, and hotel stays are covered. The Income-tax Act 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from
  1 April 2026 (TaxGuru). One source places the rule at Section 393(1); **unconfirmed**. Whether it applies to
  creator barter isn't settled in the sources. The CA decides what the page may say.
- GST: registration may be needed once a creator's income passes the threshold. The CA confirms the wording.
- The stage 3 "Tax and records" checklist.
- Once approved, admin enters the CA date in trip-page-settings and the wording shows.

## Rules checks (PM, with sources)

- **ASCI Influencer Guidelines 2025:** free stays, trips, meals, gifts and barter are a "material connection". Allowed
  labels: Ad, Advertisement, Sponsored, Collaboration, Partnership, Free gift, plus the platform tools (Instagram
  "Paid partnership", YouTube "Includes paid promotion"). Brand and creator are both responsible. **The minimum time a
  label must stay on screen in videos differs between ASCI versions**, so check the current PDF. Used in E2, S1
  terms, stage 2 posts.
- **Data sources' terms:** GOV.UK credit wording, REST Countries, Frankfurter; Agoda, Travelpayouts, GetYourGuide and
  Klook rules on how links and prices may be shown (1.35).
