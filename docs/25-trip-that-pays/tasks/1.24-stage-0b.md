## Why it matters

Where the traveller stays is part of the film and the biggest cost. Checked stays are also Pehchan's commission and
the supply for the creator-stay marketplace.

## On the page already

Checked stays for the destination: area, women-safety notes, WiFi, power backup, locker, laundry, free cancellation,
booking link (labelled affiliate), "Open in map app" link, and the two badges. The stay-mix guide and the 6-point
check. This reads straight from Strapi (1.13); the existing app's `/stays` page isn't used (1.04).

## Traveller enters

Dates, budget per night, stay style, a shortlist, the 6-point check per shortlisted stay, and any stay not on the
list (sent to admin through 1.16).

## Layout

1. **Areas first,** each with its character note (old town, market street, waterfront).
2. **Stays in the chosen area,** creator-open stays first in Creator mode.
3. **The stay mix as four slots:** base homestay or guesthouse (most nights) · hostel (1–2 nights) · one hero stay
   (1 night) · chain hotel paid with points (nights no partner covers).
4. **The 6-point check per shortlisted stay:** safe · close to your shots · real · practical · flexible · earns.

## Badges, never merged

| Badge | Traveller is told |
|---|---|
| **Pehchan-checked** | Admin has verified it. Never paid for |
| **Open to creator stays** | The stay agreed in writing to consider content-for-stay deals. Commercial. Listed first for barter pitches |

## Blocks

- **Earn:** stays booked through the page's links earn Pehchan commission; in Creator mode the traveller can use
  their own booking links in content.
- **Points:** chain-hotel nights planned as points nights.
- **Stay free:** creator-open stays first.
- **Safety:** first barter stays only where recent reviews from women travelling alone exist; meet hosts in public
  first; check the listing matches the map location.

## Map

"Open in map app" per stay, from the saved position. No embedded map (OpenStreetMap tile rules checked in 3.01).

## Later

  figures hidden until sources agree.
- Phase 3 (3.06): a labelled "AI summary of reviews" (from reviews the traveller pastes or we're allowed to use), and

## Done when, and what carries forward

Base and hero stays chosen and passing the 6-point check. Stays go to S1's stay picker, the partner tracker
(phase 2) and the booking links.
