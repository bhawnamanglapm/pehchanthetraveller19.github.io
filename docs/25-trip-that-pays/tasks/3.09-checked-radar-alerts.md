## Why it matters

A strike or a visa change mid-trip matters. The radar finds them; a person checks each before a traveller sees it.

## What to build

- `alert` collection: radar item, destination, what changed, checked by, status.
- The radar (5 sweeps a day) creates draft alerts. Admin checks each against an official source and publishes.
- Stage 2 Today view shows published alerts for the trip's destination: "Checked by Pehchan on [date]".
- Confirmed fake offers from 1.16 keep feeding the radar.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No alert shows without a checked-by name and date.
- [ ] Alerts load with the stage 2 data and are kept for offline viewing.
