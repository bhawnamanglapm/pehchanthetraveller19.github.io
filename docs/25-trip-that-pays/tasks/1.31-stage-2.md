## Why it matters

This is used on a phone, abroad, often offline, while filming. It must be quick, one-handed and safe.

## Today view (opens first)

| Section | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Deliveries due today** | From agreed S1 deals (and the partner tracker in phase 2): "Hotel X reel due tomorrow" |
| **Daily log** | Footage done · posts made (with an ad-label tick for partner posts) · money spent (local amount **and** the ₹ actually paid, by category; payment method defaults from P3) · money received · points earned (flight or stay, programme) · points used and ₹ price saved (with screenshot) · free nights used, and from which deal · deliveries done. Each updates the Trip Balance |
| **Voice input** | The browser's built-in speech recognition fills a text field. Typing always works; hide the mic where unsupported |
| **Safety card** | Emergency numbers with source and date, offline. Never AI-generated |
| **Emergency questions** | Routed to official help by the safety router copied from the existing app, or a link to the existing safety pages (decided in 1.04) |
| **Check-in nudge** | A reminder to message the trusted contact |
| **"Post after you've left"** | Beside every post: no location tags or Stories from where you're staying until you've checked out |
| **Quick consent form** | Signed on the phone, kept on the device only |
| **Booking links** | Stays and SIM cards used today, labelled affiliate. Tours from phase 2 |
| **Carried-over to-dos** | Open stage 1 items |
| **Log this flight** | From P2's main airline |

## Blocks (Creator mode)

- **Do:** film the story every day (opening, problems, people, turning point) plus B-roll; signed consent from
  everyone featured; post short clips daily; keep the money diary; deliver what was promised, on time and labelled;
  collect contacts for the next trip.
- **Earn:** paid reels, with the sourced range (Entstargate: about ₹8k–20k a reel under 30k followers, ₹20k–50k at
  30k–100k); booking links; stock footage only without identifiable people, or with signed releases.
- **Points:** every payment on the card; book direct when the hotel programme gives more than the booking site's
  discount; log every flight; rewards shopping portals.
- **Stay free:** content-for-stay on the spot, showing yesterday's reel; house-sitting and work-exchange sites
  (TrustedHousesitters, Worldpackers, Workaway) charge a yearly membership; **working for a stay on a tourist visa can
  break visa rules**, so check first.

## Bad network

Saves on the device first; loads only this stage's data; AI (phase 3) only when tapped.
