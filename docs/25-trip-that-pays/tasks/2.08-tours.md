## Why it matters

Tours earn commission (GetYourGuide 8%, Klook 5%; checked in 2.01) and give the film its scenes.

## What to build

- Labelled Travelpayouts links for GetYourGuide, Klook and Viator. A server route for Viator's Basic API only if
  prices are shown (2.01), with its key in environment variables.
- 0b: a "Tours near your base" list per destination.
- Stage 2: tours for today's area in the Today view.
- Each tour: name, price with time shown (if allowed), labelled affiliate link opening in a new tab.
- Caching and price display within each programme's rules (2.01).
- `affiliate_click` with partner and stage.

## Rules from 2.01 (checked 18 Nov 2026)

See [2.01 findings §2–3](../research/2.01-findings.md#2-caching-and-showing-prices-per-booking-programme).

- Use the Travelpayouts account (1.35) for GetYourGuide (8%, 31-day cookie), Klook (5%; 2% on special activities;
  30 days) and Viator (8%, 30 days, paid after the trip). No API is needed for links.
- Show tours as labelled links **without prices**. The exception is Viator, through its own free Basic API: cache
  its search, product and price calls for **no more than 1 hour**, and convert currency ourselves. Basic access
  can't check live availability or book.
- No scraping of any partner site. No pay-per-click ads or brand bidding for Klook.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every tour link is labelled and tracked.
- [ ] A partner API failure hides that partner's list without breaking the page.
- [ ] Display rules match the written notes from 2.01.
