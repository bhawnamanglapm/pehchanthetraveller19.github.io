## Why it matters

Insurance, eSIMs and forex cards are bought on every trip abroad. They're a commission line for Pehchan and, in E3,
for the traveller's own links. Nothing is shown until the rates are sourced.

## Steps

1. From 2.01, pick programmes with sourced commission rates.
2. Pehchan applies for its own accounts and labels its links (as 1.35).
3. Admin writes the E3 `step-guide` (how to do it, fields, done rule, feeds), following the same format as E2, and adds
   `step-link` entries.
4. Admin sets E3 to "shown". The step engine (1.26) displays it with no code change.
5. Decide with the PM whether E3 is optional (recommended) and whether the checkpoint counts it.

## Rule

P3 already says "Pehchan recommends no card". E3 must not contradict it: links are offered with sourced rates and the
traveller's own comparison, never as a recommendation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every rate shown has a source and date.
- [ ] E3 appears when switched to shown, with no deploy.
- [ ] Wording reviewed against P3 so it doesn't recommend a card.
