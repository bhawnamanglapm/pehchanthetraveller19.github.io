## Why it matters

Visitors now arrive from the main website's nav onto a separate app. The landing page explains what it is before
asking anything, and the first-visit screens set consent and expectations before anyone types a figure.

## Landing page (`/`)

The only public, indexable page besides the worked example and legal pages.

- What it is in one line (D1), and the promise: plan the place, the stays and the before, during and after, with a
  running Trip Balance.
- The two modes, side by side.
- The worked example (Pehchan's own trip) as proof, with its real Trip Balance, losses included. **Only once it
  exists (4.03):** the trip pages launch before the owner's trip, so until `workedExampleLink` is set the landing page
  shows no example and makes no claim of proof.
- "Start my trip" → first-visit screens. "Continue my trip" if a trip already exists in this browser.
- The honesty strip lines, in short.
- A link back to the main website.

## First-visit screens (`/start`), then stage 0a

| # | Screen | Holds | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What we store** | What's saved, where (this browser only), that card numbers, full membership numbers, passport details and passwords are never asked for, and that clearing site data deletes the trip. "Continue" and "Read more" (privacy page) | Consent at the start, as the DPDP Act 2023 expects. Text from trip-page-settings; lawyer-checked (1.34) |
| 2 | **Pick a mode** | Creator: Do, Earn, Points and Stay free at every stage. Just plan it safely: planner, safety and checked stays only. "You can switch later" | |
| 3 | **Your trip basics** | Home city, budget (₹), travel month, passport country, trip length | Feed 0a facts and P4's "reachable" test |
| 4 | **The honest timeline** (Creator only) | Trip 1: you pay most of it. Trips 2–3: some free stays and paid reels. After that: trips mostly pay for themselves | Labelled "a typical pattern, not a sourced figure" |

Once the worked example exists (4.03), its link sits on every screen; until then it's hidden. `trip_started` fires after screen 3 (or 4).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The landing page works without JavaScript for its text, and is indexable.
- [ ] A returning visitor with a saved trip sees "Continue my trip".
- [ ] With `workedExampleLink` empty, no worked-example link shows anywhere; with it set, it shows on every screen.
- [ ] Just plan it safely skips screen 4.
- [ ] Consent text shows the lawyer-approved version once 1.34 is done; before that, launch is blocked (1.40).
