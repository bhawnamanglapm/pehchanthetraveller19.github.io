## Why it matters

Checked stays are the first supply for the marketplace (income line 4) and the heart of 0b. The two badges must
never be confused, and Pehchan-checked must never be something a stay can buy.

## Collections and fields

| Collection | Fields |
|---|---|
| **stay** | destination, name, type (homestay, hostel, hero, chain), area, area note (character: old town, market street, waterfront), map position (latitude, longitude, looked up once), safety notes, women-solo review note, WiFi, power backup, locker, laundry, free cancellation, booking link, website, Instagram, **Pehchan-checked** (yes/no, who, date), **Open to creator stays** (yes/no, written-OK file, date), status, `checkedOn` |
| **stay-suggestion** | stay name, link, destination, what the traveller said, status (new, checked, rejected), date received |

## Rules

- **Open to creator stays** can't be set to yes without the written-OK file and its date (1.07 rule 2).
- **Pehchan-checked** has no relationship to any payment, fee or sponsorship field, now or later (1.07 rule 3). If
  income line 4 (2.13) adds a listing fee, it goes on a separate record, never on this field.
- A stay suggestion never becomes a stay automatically. Admin runs the stay check (1.38 runbook, admin §4) and
  creates a draft `stay`.
- Map position: looked up by hand, once per stay, through Nominatim at no more than 1 request a second. Never in
  bulk, never as the traveller types. The page links out to a map app; there's no embedded map (README §4).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both collections exist with the fields above.
- [ ] Setting the creator badge without the file is refused.
- [ ] A suggestion from the public form (1.16) arrives as status "new" and can't be published as a stay.
