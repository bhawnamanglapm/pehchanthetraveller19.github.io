## Why it matters

On-page reminders only work when the page is open. Email reminders help travellers keep promises to partners.

## What to build

- An opt-in: the traveller gives an email and ticks "Send me reminders". Off by default.
- Reminders: delivery due (from the tracker), follow-up due on a pitch with no reply, departure in 14 days with
  required steps open.
- Sent through Resend from the server. Phase 2 has no accounts, so the browser sends only what's needed for each
  reminder (email, date, short text). The server keeps it only until it's sent, then deletes it.
- Every email has a one-click stop link.
- The consent screen and privacy policy are updated, and the lawyer checks the change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No email is stored after its reminder is sent or cancelled.
- [ ] The stop link works with no login.
- [ ] Privacy text updated and approved before release.
