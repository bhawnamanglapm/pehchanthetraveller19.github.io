## Two stay checks, both used

- **The 6-point check** (README §4): safe, close to your shots, real, practical, flexible, earns. Travellers see it
  in 0b, and admin §3 refers to it.
- **Admin's seven steps** (admin §4): how admin verifies those six points. Admin §4 has no step for "close to your
  shots", so 1.36 and 1.38 add the walking distance to the sunrise and sunset spots to step 1.
