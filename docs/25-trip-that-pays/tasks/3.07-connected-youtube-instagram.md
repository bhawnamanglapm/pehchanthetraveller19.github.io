## Why it matters

Typed follower numbers with screenshots work, but connected numbers are trusted by partners and save time.

## What to build

- Connect YouTube (YouTube Analytics API) and Instagram (needs a professional account and Meta app review) through
  OAuth, from the media kit.
- Pull follower counts, views and reach into the media kit and partner reports, labelled "Connected, [date]".
- Store only what the rules allow: YouTube's 30-day refresh rule unless 3.01 finds otherwise.
- Disconnect at any time; disconnecting deletes the stored numbers and tokens.
- Tokens encrypted at rest.

## Rules from 3.01 (checked 18 Nov 2026)

From the [3.01 findings](../research/3.01-findings.md), items 1 and 2.

**Instagram**
- Use Instagram API with Instagram Login (professional accounts; no Facebook Page needed). Permissions:
  `instagram_business_basic` and `instagram_business_manage_insights`.
- Standard Access works for accounts with a role on the app, so build and test on the owner's account first.
- **Advanced Access** (every other traveller) needs App Review and Business Verification. Review needs:
  - at least one successful API call;
  - one screencast per permission;
  - test login steps;
  - a privacy policy URL.
- Some metrics are missing on accounts under 100 followers. Meta keeps account metrics for 90 days only.

**YouTube** (numbers read with the creator's sign-in are Authorized Data)
- They may be stored as long as needed, but **check every 30 days that the authorisation is still valid**. If it isn't,
  delete the stored numbers.
- **Deletion:**
  - our own disconnect button: within 7 days;
  - revoked in Google's settings: within 30 days;
  - a deletion request: within 7 days.
- Show every number with its date.
- No home-made metrics from YouTube data (III.E.4.h).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Meta app review approved before Instagram goes live.
- [ ] Stored numbers follow the written storage rules.
