## Why it matters

Booking commission is income line 1 and the only income from day one. Without approved accounts there are no
tracking links, and without the disclosure the links break the rules.

Note the difference: these are **Pehchan's** accounts, used on stay booking links and stage 2 booking links. In E2,
the **traveller** joins programmes with their own accounts for their own posts.

## Steps

1. Apply to Agoda, Travelpayouts, GetYourGuide and Klook (or the network each one runs through). Record date applied
   and status.
2. Read each programme's rules on: where links may appear, required labels, showing prices, caching, cookie length,
   and whether links can be used in an app on a subdomain. Write down each with its source link.
3. Once approved, create links for the launch destination's stays (1.36) and stage 2 items, and enter them in Strapi.
4. Label every affiliate link on the page ("Affiliate: we may earn a commission at no cost to you", final wording
   from 1.34) and link to `/legal/affiliate-disclosure`.
5. Set up the monthly commission entry in `commission-log` with a screenshot from each dashboard (1.12, 1.38).

## Rates shown to travellers

Only rates with sources: Agoda about 4–7% (track360 teardown), GetYourGuide about 8% (Travelpayouts listing), Klook
5–6.5% (Involve Asia). Insurance, eSIM and forex rates aren't shown until sourced (2.09). Anything not sourced isn't
shown.
