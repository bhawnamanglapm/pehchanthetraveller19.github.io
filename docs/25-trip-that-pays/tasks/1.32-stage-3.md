## Why it matters

This is where one trip turns into repeat deals, a guide that earns for years, and the proof of the Trip Balance.

## Sections

| Section | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Partner reports** | One per partner from the template, filled from the traveller's typed numbers: views, reach, clicks, screenshots |
| **Edit plan** | Long version for YouTube or festivals plus 10–20 short clips |
| **"How to do this trip" guide** | Costs from the money diary, stays, mistakes, with the traveller's booking links |
| **Media kit update** | New numbers go into the media kit for the next trip |
| **Festivals and platforms** | A checklist for submitting the film |
| **Tax and records** | What to give a CA: free stays received, paid deals, money received. Wording shown only after the CA date is set |
| **Feedback** | On each stay and partner, sent to admin (through the stay-suggestion route or a small feedback form under 1.16's rules) |
| **"Feature my trip"** | Phase 3 (3.10) |
| **Final Trip Balance** | Closes with the real net; `trip_closed` fires |
| **Next place** | A new trip at 0a, carrying this trip's numbers into the media kit and pitches |

## Blocks (Creator mode)

- **Earn:** YouTube Partner Programme (one route: 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours in 12 months, with its
  source), paid repeat deals, digital products (paid itinerary, preset pack, plan-your-own-trip guide), licensing to
  platforms, airlines or tourism boards, workshops and talks.
- **Points:** redeem for the next trip's flight; hotel points for nights no partner covers (tips from P2).
- **Stay free:** pitch bigger hotels with a finished film and real numbers.

## Done when

Film out, partner reports sent, guide live.
