## Why it matters

Everything on the page is only as good as the checking behind it. The runbook turns the admin plan into a routine
someone else could follow on a bad day.

## What the runbook contains

**1. Adding a destination.** The ten items and the timing sheet (1.36).

**2. Checking a stay.** Two lists, both used:
- **The 6-point check** (README §4, the same one travellers see in 0b): safe, close to your shots, real, practical,
  flexible, earns. Admin §3 #7 refers to this one.
- **Admin's seven steps** (admin §4): how admin verifies those points (map position once, guest photos, women's
  reviews and safety note, WiFi, power, locker, laundry and cancellation confirmed, booking link labelled,
  Pehchan-checked, creator badge with written OK).

Admin §4 has no step for "close to your shots" (walking distance to sunrise and sunset spots). The runbook adds it to
step 1, when the map position is saved.

**3. Queues, with what to do for each**

| Queue | Action |
|---|---|
| Fake offers | Check the stay's official site and phone; mark confirmed fake or not fake; confirmed fakes go to the radar and can become a stage 1 warning |
| Stay suggestions | Run the stay check, or reject |
| Broken links (weekly email) | Fix the URL and `checkedOn`, or hide a programme or stay that's gone |
| Stale facts (past 30 days) | Recheck against the source and update `checkedOn` |
| Advisory changes (daily) | Read the change; update visa or permit notes if needed |
| Flagged AI drafts (phase 3) | Find the cause, fix, rerun the evals, add an eval case, or mark "not a fault" |

**4. The monthly routine (one session)**

1. Recheck everything in the "past 30 days" filter.
2. Update the dong rate and confirm the fixed rates.
3. Work the fake-offer, stay-suggestion and flagged-draft queues.
4. Check new stays for the next destination.
5. Enter last month's commission from each network's dashboard, with screenshots (`commission-log`).
6. Look at the most-skipped steps (`step_skipped`) and fix the guide text or link behind the top one.
7. If AI is on: read five random drafts per AI tool from the eval log (not travellers' trips); check AI cost against
   the budget.
8. Confirm last month's Strapi backup exists and opens.
9. Phase 3: showcases, disputes, deletion requests.

**5. Yearly and quarterly.** Yearly: festivals and climate months for every destination. Quarterly: review the 0a
scoring weights, writing down why any weight changed.

**6. When something breaks.** Restore Strapi from backup (tested in 1.06); what to do if the link-check, advisory or
rate jobs fail; who to call.

**7. Rules the system enforces** (1.07), listed so admin knows why a publish is refused.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The runbook is a single document in the project folder, linked from the Strapi admin welcome note.
- [ ] A helper who hasn't seen it before completes one stay check and one queue item using only the runbook.
- [ ] The first monthly session is run and timed.
