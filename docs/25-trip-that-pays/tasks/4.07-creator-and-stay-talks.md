## Why it matters

**Moved here on 16 Nov 2026 (owner).** This was phase 0 task 0.05. The talks now happen after launch, so creators and
stays can try the live pages instead of imagining them. **The cost:** demand, barter terms and prices (assumptions
A1–A5 in pm-lifecycle §1) are untested at launch.

The page sells to two sides. These talks give real demand, real barter terms and real prices, instead of guesses.

## What to ask creators (10)

- How do you choose a place today? Show me the last time.
- What did your last trip cost, and what did it earn? What did you get free?
- Have you pitched a stay? What did you offer, what came back, was it in writing?
- Have you had a fake collaboration offer?
- Would you use a page like this? Which stage matters most? (After launch: open the live pages together and note
  where they stop and what they skip.)
- Would you pay for AI drafts, synced accounts and automatic partner reports? Show two price points (set by the owner
  before the talks) and record the reaction to each. Don't suggest a "right" answer.

## What to ask stays (10)

- Have creators asked you for free nights? What did you get back?
- Would you agree in writing to consider content-for-stay deals?
- Would you pay to be listed as "open to creator stays"? Would you rather pay per confirmed collaboration?
- What would make you trust a creator?

## Rules

- Record answers as said, with the date. Don't round them into a story.
- Never promise a listing, a badge or a deal.
- Pehchan-checked is never offered in exchange for anything.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 10 creator and 10 stay notes, dated, in one place.
- [ ] A one-page tally: how many would use it, how many would pay and at which price point, how many stays would sign
      a written OK, and which listing model stays prefer.
