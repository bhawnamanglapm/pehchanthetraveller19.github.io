## Why it matters

Hotels and tourism boards ask "how many travellers do you send?" Real totals win listings (income line 4) and
sponsored guides (line 5). They must never identify anyone.

## What to build

- An opt-in tick at trip close: "Count my trip, without my name, in Pehchan's totals." Off by default.
  `pitch_totals_opt_in` fires.
- If ticked, the browser sends only: destination, nights booked through the page, number of creator-stay deals done,
  month. No name, no email, no stay names unless the stay itself agreed.
- Admin sees totals per destination, never single trips. Totals under a minimum count (for example 5 trips) are not
  shown, so a single traveller can't be worked out.
- Privacy text updated and lawyer-checked.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Nothing is sent without the tick.
- [ ] Admin's view shows totals only, hiding small counts.
- [ ] The privacy text covers it before release.
