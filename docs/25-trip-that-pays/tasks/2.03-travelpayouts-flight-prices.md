## Why it matters

0a question 4 ("Can my audience afford it?") needs the flight price from the traveller's home city. It also earns
commission when the traveller books through the link.

## What to build

| Part | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Route** | `GET /api/trip/flights?from=&to=&month=`, server-side, key in `TRAVELPAYOUTS_TOKEN` |
| **Cache** | A few hours, within Travelpayouts' rules from 2.01 |
| **On the page** | Cheapest found fare for the travel month beside each candidate place: "Prices from [time], may have changed". A labelled affiliate link to book |
| **Scoring** | Feeds question 4 together with admin's daily cost range and the traveller's budget. The formula stays in `score0a.ts` |
| **Failure** | Hide the price and let the traveller score question 4 by hand |

Flight prices are never generated by AI.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every price shows its time and the "may have changed" note.
- [ ] Caching and display follow the written rules from 2.01.
- [ ] `affiliate_click` fires with partner "Travelpayouts" and stage "0a".
