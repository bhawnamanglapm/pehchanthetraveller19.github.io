## What to build

- For each "delivered" tracker row, the partner report fills itself with the connected numbers for the posts linked
  to that partner: views, reach, clicks, screenshots.
- An AI summary paragraph (3.06) from those real numbers, labelled as a draft.
- Export as PDF or a share link; the traveller sends it. Marking it sent moves the tracker row to "reported".

## Rules from 3.01 (checked 18 Nov 2026)

From the [3.01 findings](../research/3.01-findings.md), item 2:
- Show YouTube's numbers as returned, each with its date.
- **Don't work out new figures from YouTube data** (for example engagement rate or growth %). YouTube policy III.E.4.h
  forbids it, unless Google accepts an application under its Derived Metrics policy. Applying is free; decide here.
- Instagram account metrics are kept by Meta for 90 days. Reports that cover longer need our own stored copies.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every number in a report traces to a connected account or a typed number with a screenshot.
- [ ] Nothing is sent automatically.
