| Stage | Feature | Label | Notes |

Every draft has "Flag this draft".

## Switching on

One stage at a time, after its evals pass, in this order: 0a explanations, stage 1 pitches and captions, stage 3
reports (admin §9), then the rest.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each feature shows its label and works by hand when AI is off or the limit is reached.
- [ ] Voice-to-log never saves without the traveller confirming.
- [ ] Each stage is switched on only after its evals pass, with the date recorded.
