## Why it matters

The paid Creator tier (AI drafts beyond the free limit, sync, connected numbers, automatic partner reports) has no
price yet. Two price points are tested before anything is built for payment.

## What to do

- The owner sets two price points. They aren't set in the plan.
- A "Creator tier: coming soon" panel shows what's included and one of the two prices (split evenly by browser).
  "I'm interested" leaves an email (optional) or just a click.
- No payment is taken. Interest is recorded, with the price shown, in Strapi or the analytics tool.
- Run for a set period (the owner chooses), then compare interest per price.
- Leads who ask questions go through the existing contact form (admin §10). The trip app is a separate site, so it
  links out to that form rather than copying it; the PM confirms which site's form is used.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each browser sees one price consistently.
- [ ] Records show price shown, click, and optional email only.
- [ ] A written result with the chosen price goes into 3.11.
