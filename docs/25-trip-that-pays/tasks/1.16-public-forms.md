## Why it matters

These are the only places a traveller sends anything to Pehchan in phases 1–2. They must be safe to leave open to
the public and must collect nothing personal.

## Forms

| Form | Route | Lands in | Fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Report a fake offer** (S1, stage 1 safety) | `POST /api/report-offer` | `offer-report` | What was offered, stay or brand name, link, how it reached them, date |
| **A stay not on our list** (0b) | `POST /api/suggest-stay` | `stay-suggestion` | Stay name, link, destination, what the traveller said |
| **Flag this draft** (phase 3) | `POST /api/flag-draft` | `ai-flag` | Which tool, the draft text, what was wrong |

## Rules for all three

- No name, email or phone field.
- Rate-limited per IP address (proposed: 5 a day per form). The IP is used for the limit only and never stored with
  the report.
- Links checked against an allowed list of shapes (`https` only; no shortened links); anything else is refused with a
  plain message.
- Text length capped; HTML stripped.
- Written with the write-only token (1.05), which can create these entries and nothing else.
- The traveller sees a plain confirmation: what was sent, and that nothing else from their trip was.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each form creates one entry with status "new" and no personal data.
- [ ] The sixth submission in a day from one address is refused with a plain message.
- [ ] A non-https or shortened link is refused.
