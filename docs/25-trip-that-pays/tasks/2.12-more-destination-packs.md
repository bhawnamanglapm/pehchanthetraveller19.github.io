## Why it matters

Each new destination widens who the page serves and adds booking commission. The pace is set by how fast admin can
check facts and stays, never faster: about one destination a month is the plan, adjusted by the first pack's timing.

## Target

India and all five corridor destinations (Dubai/UAE, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Nepal), about six months after
launch at one a month (README §1). Vietnam needs the monthly dong rate; Dubai/UAE and Nepal use the fixed rates.

## How

- Order destinations by what travellers pick in 0a (from analytics) and the candidate list from phase 0.
- Same ten items and stay check as 1.36, with Hindi versions (2.06).
- Each destination appears only when every item is published, sourced and dated.
- Note each pack's time to keep the monthly pace honest.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each new pack meets every item in 1.36.
- [ ] The time per pack is written down.
