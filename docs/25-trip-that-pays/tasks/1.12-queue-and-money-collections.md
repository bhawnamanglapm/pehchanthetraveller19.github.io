## Why it matters

The fake-offer queue protects travellers and feeds the radar. The commission log is the first income line's only
record, and it must always have a source.

## Collections and fields

| Collection | Fields |
|---|---|
| **offer-report** | what was offered, stay or brand name, link, how it reached them (Instagram DM, WhatsApp, email, other), date, status (new, confirmed fake, not fake), sent-to-radar date |
| **commission-log** | month, network (Agoda, Travelpayouts, GetYourGuide, Klook, others as joined), clicks, bookings, amount, currency, screenshot of the network's dashboard |

## Rules

- `offer-report` stores no name, email, phone or IP address of the reporter (1.16).
- A confirmed fake gets a sent-to-radar date when admin passes it on, and can become a warning line in the stage 1
  fake-offer checklist (through `step-guide` S1 text).
- `commission-log` amounts are typed from each network's own dashboard, with the screenshot attached. No estimate is
  ever entered.

Later queue collections (`ai-flag`, `alert`, `showcase`, `dispute`, `deletion-request`) are built in phase 3 tasks
3.04, 3.05, 3.09 and 3.10.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both collections exist with the fields above.
- [ ] A report from the public form arrives with status "new" and no personal data.
- [ ] A commission row can't be saved without its screenshot.
