## Why it matters

Many of the travellers Pehchan serves read Hindi more comfortably. Safety and money guidance only helps if it's
understood.

## What to build

- `/hi/...` routes using the `/hi/` setup copied from the existing app (1.04), with a language switch on every page.
- Strapi localisation turned on for: templates, step guides, honesty strip, and the destination facts travellers read
  (visa note, permit note, climate, festivals, emergency number labels).
- Interface text in Hindi, reviewed by a fluent reader, not just machine-translated.
- The consent form is already in Hindi from phase 1 (1.34).
- Same accessibility and reading-level check as English (1.33).

## Rule

A Hindi fact has its own `checkedOn` date. If the English fact is updated and the Hindi isn't, the Hindi page shows
the amber "check the official site" warning.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every screen of the app works in Hindi, including stage 2 offline.
- [ ] A Hindi fact older than its English version shows amber.
- [ ] Analytics events are the same in both languages.
