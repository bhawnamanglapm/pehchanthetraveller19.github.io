## What it includes

AI drafts beyond the free daily limit, synced accounts, connected Instagram and YouTube numbers, automatic partner
reports.

## What to build

- Built on the existing `/trip-plan/premium` and Razorpay pattern (copied in 1.04). Keys in environment variables.
- The price chosen from 2.11.
- The free planner stays free: every phase 1 feature keeps working without paying.
- Receipts and cancellation; terms updated and lawyer-checked.

## Rules from 3.01 (checked 18 Nov 2026)

From the [3.01 findings](../research/3.01-findings.md), item 6:
- **First version:** a one-off "period pass" (for example 30 days) on the existing Orders pattern:
  - 2% fee plus 18% GST on the fee;
  - no mandate setup.
- **Auto-renewal later**, if wanted: Razorpay Subscriptions.
  - Card subscriptions cost 0.9% plus the 2% platform fee per charge. UPI and eMandate pricing is on request.
  - Needs Flash Checkout enabled.
  - Uses the `subscription.*` webhook events.
- The premium plan's ₹199–499 price check doesn't apply. Give the tier its own price setting (from 2.11).
- Tier and payment records go in the traveller database (3.02), not Strapi.
