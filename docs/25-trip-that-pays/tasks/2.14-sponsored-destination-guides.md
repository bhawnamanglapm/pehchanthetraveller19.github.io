## Why it matters

Tourism boards pay to reach travellers planning their country. A sponsored guide earns without weakening the facts.

## How

- Pitch tourism boards using traffic, anonymous totals and the worked example.
- A sponsored destination shows a clear **"Sponsored"** label on its guide and cards.
- The sponsor can supply story ideas, photos and offers. **Visa and safety facts stay admin-checked, and the sponsor
  can't edit them** (enforced in Strapi, 1.07).
- Sponsored places are never scored higher in 0a. The formula ignores sponsorship.
- Tourism boards that ask about a sponsored guide come through the existing contact form (admin §10), linked from the
  trip app, the same as in 2.11.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The label shows everywhere the destination appears.
- [ ] A test shows the sponsor role can't edit visa, permit or emergency fields.
- [ ] 0a scores are the same with sponsorship on or off.
