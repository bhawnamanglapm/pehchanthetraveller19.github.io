## Why it matters

Templates, page text and the 0a scoring weights change more often than code. Keeping them in Strapi means a fix is
a publish, and the legal gates (lawyer date, CA date) are enforced in one place.

## Collections and fields

| Collection | Fields |
|---|---|
| **template** | name (shot list, media kit, hotel pitch, tourism-board pitch, consent form, points plan, partner report, guide page), language (English; Hindi in phase 2), body, lawyer-checked date (consent form only), `checkedOn` |
| **trip-page-settings** (single entry) | honesty strip lines (each with its "why" text), honest timeline text (with the label "a typical pattern, not a sourced figure"), worked example link, CA-approved date for tax wording, **0a scoring weights** (one per question, with date changed and why), AI on/off per stage (0a, 0b, 1, 2, 3; all off at launch), AI daily limit, consent-screen text ("What we store"), clear-data warning text |

## Content to write for launch

| Template | Source of the content |
|---|---|
| Shot list | What worked on Pehchan's own trip (4.02; until then, a first draft from the 0.05 talks): day-by-day scenes, people to meet, places that need permission, B-roll list |
| Media kit | One page: who you are, the story, channels and numbers (with screenshot), past work |
| Hotel pitch | Specific deliverables and dates, media kit and one reel attached, the ad label |
| Tourism-board pitch | The 0a story angle, Indian audience, dates, deliverables, a request for local introductions |
| Consent form | **English and Hindi, lawyer-checked.** Covers children (parent or guardian consent, no face close-ups without it) and women filmed in public (ask first, blur anyone who didn't agree) |
| Points plan | Main airline, main chain, expiry rules with sources, which nights are points nights |
| Partner report | Views, reach, clicks, screenshots, what was delivered and when |
| Guide page | Costs from the money diary, stays, mistakes, the traveller's own booking links |

The consent form is entered in Hindi in phase 1 because the plan requires it in both languages; the rest of Hindi
waits for 2.06.

## Rules

- The consent form can't publish without the lawyer-checked date (1.07 rule 4).
- Tax wording in the honesty strip and stage 3 stays hidden until the CA-approved date is set (1.07 rule 5).
- Weights changes are dated with a reason; the quarterly review (1.38) reads this history.
