## Why it matters

These show in **both modes**. They're what makes the film sellable and the traveller safe, whether or not they earn
from the trip.

## Do checklist (4–8 weeks out)

| Item | What the page gives |
|---|---|
| Story and shot list | The template (day-by-day scenes, people to meet, places that need permission). AI draft from the story angle in phase 3 |
| Filming and drone rules | The destination's admin permit note, dated |
| Music | The film needs music it's licensed to use: the platform's own library, a paid licence, or original music. Background music recorded at a location can trigger a copyright claim |
| Filming on private property | Ask the owner first (stays, cafés, shops, temples). Get a written yes for anything in a paid post |
| Media kit | Phase 1: the template. Phase 2: the builder (2.05). Follower numbers typed in with a screenshot until phase 3 |
| Pitch hotels, homestays, tour operators and the tourism board | Creator mode: a link to the S1 (1.29) and S3 cards, and the item ticks itself when S1 is Done. **Not settled by the plan:** the Do list shows in both modes, but the S1 and S3 cards are hidden in Just plan it safely mode. The PM decides with the owner whether this item shows there as a plain tick box or is hidden |
| Consent form | English and Hindi, lawyer-checked. Children: a parent's or guardian's consent, no face close-ups without it. Women filmed in public: ask first, blur anyone who didn't agree |
| Insurance that covers camera gear | A reminder and what to look for |
| "I'm going to X, what should I film?" post | A prompt |

Each item can be ticked; the checkpoint (1.30) reads the ticks.

## Safety (every mode)

| Item | What the page does |
|---|---|
| **Fake-offer checklist** | Verify through the hotel's official website and phone number; never pay to collaborate (no "registration fee"); share ID only at check-in, as the law requires; no passport scans by message. "Report a fake offer" button (1.16) |
| **Someone knows your plan** | Share the itinerary with one trusted contact, using the share approach copied from the existing trip-plan (1.04) |
| **Emergency card** | Prepared now from the destination's emergency numbers and saved offline for stage 2 (1.18) |
| **Insurance** | The camera-gear reminder above |

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every Do item and safety item shows in both modes.
- [ ] The consent form opens in English and Hindi.
- [ ] Sharing produces a link or file the contact can open without the app.
- [ ] The emergency card works offline after "Save offline".
