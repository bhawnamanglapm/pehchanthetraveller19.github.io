## Showcases

- Stage 3 "Feature my trip" sends a trip summary, film link, ad labels shown and a consent-confirmed tick to the
  `showcase` collection.
- Admin checks it's honest, ads are labelled and consent is shown, then publishes it as a worked example (`/example`
  can list several) and uses it on Instagram with the traveller's OK.

## Disputes

- `dispute` collection: stay, what was agreed, what happened, evidence, outcome, badge removed (yes/no).
- Either side can report: a stay that broke a deal, or a traveller who didn't deliver. Through a form under the same
  rules as 1.16.
- Admin reviews the written terms. Pehchan isn't a party to the deal (terms, 1.34); the outcome is about the creator
  badge and the record, not money.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No showcase is published without the consent tick and an admin check.
- [ ] A dispute outcome that removes a badge updates the stay at once.
