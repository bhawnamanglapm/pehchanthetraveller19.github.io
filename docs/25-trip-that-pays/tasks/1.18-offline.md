## Why it matters

Stage 2 happens abroad, often on poor mobile data. The safety card and the daily log must work with no connection.

## What to build

- A service worker that caches the app shell and the stage 2 screens.
- **Save offline** in the trip menu: caches the current destination's emergency numbers, stays, today's deliveries
  and the trip itself for offline use. Copy the approach from the existing app's offline page (1.04).
- Stage 2 loads only its own data.
- The log saves to the device first. There's nothing to sync in phases 1–2 because there's no server copy; phase 3
  adds sync (3.03).
- A clear offline banner. Anything that needs the network (booking links, report forms, AI in phase 3) says so
  instead of failing silently.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With the network off after "Save offline", stage 2 opens, the safety card shows numbers with source and date,
      and log entries save.
- [ ] On a throttled "slow 3G" profile, stage 2's first load stays usable (target agreed with the designer).
