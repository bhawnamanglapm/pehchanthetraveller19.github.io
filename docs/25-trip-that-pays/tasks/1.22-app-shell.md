## Why it matters

These parts sit on every stage. The Trip Balance is the hook and must be exact and honest.

## Trip Card (top of every stage)

| Part | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Destination, dates, "why" line | From 0a. Edit goes back to 0a |
| **Trip Balance** | Spent · earned · saved with points · free nights · **net**. Agreed money and agreed nights show in grey as "expected". Only money received and nights used count in the net. A negative net shows plainly, never softened |
| Current stage and next step | For example "Stage 1 · Next: send 3 more pitches". Tapping opens that step |
| Mode | Switching hides or shows Earn, Points and Stay free; nothing typed is lost |
| To-dos | Open items carried over when a stage moved on by date. Each links to its step |

## Trip Balance: where each figure comes from (nothing is estimated)

| Figure | From |
|---|---|
| **Spent** | Money diary: the ₹ actually paid, from the card or forex statement, not the local price converted |
| **Earned** | E1 pre-sales and sponsors received, paid deals (partner tracker from phase 2), commission the traveller copies from their network dashboard |
| **Saved with points** | Each redemption: points used, plus the ₹ price otherwise paid, with a screenshot |
| **Free nights** | Barter nights used (S1 and stage 2 on-the-spot deals). Valued at the room's normal price if entered, otherwise a count |
| **Net** | Earned + saved with points + value of free nights − spent |

Write `tripBalance.ts` as plain functions with unit tests for every row, including a loss and a trip with only
counted (unvalued) nights.

## Stage stepper

`0a Choose place → 0b Choose stays → 1 Before → 2 During → 3 After`. One panel at a time. Earlier stages stay
editable; going back to 0b from stage 1 loses nothing. Stage 1 → 2 moves on the departure date and 2 → 3 on the
return date, automatically.

**A missed check never blocks the traveller** (README §8). Nothing on the page refuses to move on because a step or
check is unfinished; it shows as a to-do on the Trip Card instead. **Nothing is typed twice:** the "why" goes into
pitches, stays into the tracker, the money diary into the Trip Balance and the "real cost" post, deliveries into the
reports.

## Every stage panel

Same blocks: **Do · Earn · Points · Stay free**, with that stage's safety items inside. Just plan it safely shows only
Do and safety. Each empty block says what goes there and links to the template or worked example.

## Honesty strip (always visible)

From trip-page-settings, each line with a "why" link:
- Label every partner post; free stays, meals and barter count as a paid relationship under ASCI rules.
- Free stays can be taxable, so check with a CA (hidden until the CA date is set).
- Get consent before filming people.

## Trip menu

Export my trip · Import · Share with a trusted contact (1.25) · Save offline (1.18) · Delete my trip · Next place
(after stage 3: new trip at 0a, carrying this trip's numbers into the media kit).
