## Why it matters

A creator with five stays and two paid reels needs one place to see what's owed to whom and by when. Missed
deliveries end repeat deals.

## What to build

- One row per partner, moving **pitched → agreed → delivered → reported**.
- Each row: deal type (barter or paid), what's owed, by when, the ad label to use, the written terms (linked from S1),
  and notes.
- **Rows appear automatically:** sponsor rows from E1, agreed rows from S1 and S3. Nothing is typed twice.
- Stage 2 "Deliveries due today" reads from the tracker instead of S1 directly.
- Stage 3 partner reports start from the tracker's "delivered" rows; marking a report sent moves the row to
  "reported".
- `deal_logged` fires when a row reaches agreed (barter or paid).
- Saved in IndexedDB with the rest of the trip, and included in export.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An S1 pitch marked agreed appears in the tracker with its terms.
- [ ] A row can't move to agreed without written terms (same rule as S1).
- [ ] Deliveries due today and tomorrow show in the stage 2 Today view.
- [ ] Export and import keep tracker rows.
