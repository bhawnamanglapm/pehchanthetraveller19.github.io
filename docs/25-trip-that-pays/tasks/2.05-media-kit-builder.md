## Why it matters

Every pitch (S1, S3, E1) needs a media kit. A builder turns the traveller's numbers and the 0a "why" into a
one-page PDF, instead of a blank template.

## What to build

- **Inputs:** name or handle, channels, follower numbers per channel with a screenshot (typed by the traveller until
  phase 3), sample work links, the 0a "why" line as the story angle, past partners (from the tracker, with
  permission), rates (optional).
- **Output:** a one-page PDF and a shareable image, made in the browser. Two or three designs.
- **Feeds:** S2 eligibility (follower numbers), S1 and S3 pitches (link), stage 3 "Media kit update" (new numbers
  after the trip).
- Numbers come only from the traveller (and connected accounts from phase 3), never from AI.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The PDF is readable on a phone and prints on one A4 page.
- [ ] A follower number without a screenshot shows "not verified" in the kit.
- [ ] Stage 3 numbers update the kit for the next trip without retyping.
