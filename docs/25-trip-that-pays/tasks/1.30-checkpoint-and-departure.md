## Why it matters

It tells the traveller what's left without ever stopping them from travelling.

## Screen (`/trip/1/ready`)

One row per Do item, safety item and guided step: status, what's missing, "Go to step". An overall colour at the top,
with text, not colour alone.

## Colour rules (checked in this order, recalculated after any change)

| Colour | When |
|---|---|
| **Red** | An agreed barter deal has no written terms, **or** departure is 14 days away or less (threshold from `step-guide`) and a required step isn't started |
| **Green** | Every required step is Done or Not needed, and every optional step is Done or Skipped with a reason |
| **Amber** | Anything else (a required step in progress, an optional step neither done nor skipped) |

Just plan it safely: only Do and safety items count.

## On the departure date

- The stage moves to 2 anyway.
- Open required steps become Trip Card to-dos and stage 2 daily items (for example "Terms for Stay X still not in
  writing").
- `checkpoint_status` fires once with the colour.
- The checkpoint keeps updating when steps are finished later.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Write `checkpoint.ts` with tests for every row, including a deal without terms 30 days out (red) and all
      required done with one optional untouched (amber).
- [ ] Changing the departure date to today moves the stage and creates the to-dos.
- [ ] Nothing on the screen blocks moving on.
