## Why it matters

The nav link, the Strapi webhook, the affiliate programmes and the legal pages all need a fixed address.

**Changed 15 Nov 2026 (owner, D2):** there's no subdomain. The address is `www.pehchanthetraveller.com/trip/`, on
the main site's existing HTTPS and Vercel project, so no DNS work is needed. The table below is the original plan; the
search, sitemap and title rows still apply to the `/trip/` pages.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `www.pehchanthetraveller.com/trip/` serves the pages over HTTPS once `tripApp` is on.
- [ ] Preview deployments are not indexable; a traveller's own stage pages are `noindex`.
- [ ] The sitemap lists only public trip pages (`/trip/`), and only while `tripApp` is on. The worked example stays
      out until 4.03.
