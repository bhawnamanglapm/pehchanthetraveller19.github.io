## Why it matters

It's the proof. A link to it sits on every first-visit screen and every empty block. It shows the real numbers,
losses included, so the page's promise is honest.

## What `/example` shows

- The trip from 0a to 3: why the place was picked (the 0a scores), the stays used, the pitches sent and their replies,
  the deals agreed with their terms (details anonymised where a partner didn't agree to be named).
- The real Trip Balance: spent, earned, saved with points, free nights, and the net, even if it's negative.
- What went wrong and what would be done differently.
- Links to the film and Instagram posts, with ad labels as posted.
- Consent: anyone shown has signed the consent form; children and people who didn't agree are blurred or removed.

## Rules

- No invented numbers. Every figure comes from the phase 0 sheet with screenshots.
- Updated when a later trip gives a better example (phase 3 showcases can become worked examples, 3.10).
- Indexable (1.02), with Instagram-friendly share images.
- The same story is Pehchan's travel tech fest talk (income line 2), so keep a slide-ready version of the Trip
  Balance and the 0a scores.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every number traces to the phase 0 sheet.
- [ ] The page passes the same accessibility checks as the app (1.33).
- [ ] The link set in trip-page-settings opens it from every first-visit screen.
