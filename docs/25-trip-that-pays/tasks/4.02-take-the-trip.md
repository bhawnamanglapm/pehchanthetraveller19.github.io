## Why it matters

It's the proof: the worked example (4.03) that the trip pages link to once it's real. It also tests stages 1–3 on a
real trip, so the page can be fixed to match what really happened.

**Moved here on 16 Nov 2026 (owner).** This was phase 0 task 0.06. The owner can't travel to every place yet and wants
the trip pages to launch first, so the trip is now the last step of the plan. Until it's done, the pages launch
without a worked example, and the worked-example links stay hidden.

## What to do

By now the trip pages are live, so plan and log the trip **on the pages themselves**. Keep a Google Sheet (or Notion)
only for what the pages can't hold yet, plus every receipt and screenshot:

- **Stage 1:** every guided step (E1, E2, P1–P4, S1–S3) with the outputs listed in user-page §6, and the
  "Ready for the trip?" colour worked out by hand on the departure date.
- **Stage 2:** the daily log: footage, posts (with an ad-label tick), money spent (local amount and the ₹ actually
  paid), money received, points earned and used, free nights used, deliveries.
- **Stage 3:** partner reports, the "how to do this trip" guide, the final Trip Balance.

Keep the Trip Balance exactly as defined in README §2: spent, earned, saved with points, value of free nights, net.
If the net is negative, record it as negative.

## What to record for the next build

- Which steps were confusing, skipped, or needed a field the plan doesn't have.
- Which stage-2 log entries were hard to make one-handed.
- Where the pages or the sheet failed (offline, photos, sharing).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The trip is recorded from 0a to 3 with real numbers and screenshots.
- [ ] Final Trip Balance written down, with every figure traceable to a receipt or screenshot.
- [ ] A list of changes the build should make, handed to the PM.
