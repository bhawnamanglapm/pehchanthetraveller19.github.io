## Why it matters

Every fact the traveller sees in 0a, stage 1 and the stage 2 safety card that isn't from a live API comes from these
collections. Each one carries its source and the date it was checked, so the page can show both and turn amber after
30 days.

## Collections and fields

Every collection below has `checkedOn` (date), `source` (link) where it holds a fact, and Draft & Publish on.

| Collection | Fields | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **destination** | name, slug, country, `inIndia` (yes/no), daily cost range (₹ min, ₹ max, sources), tourism-board note, `tourismBoardMediaContact`, creator-friendly stays note, dong rate with source (Vietnam only), status, `sponsored` (yes/no, sponsor name; phase 2, task 2.14) | `inIndia` makes P3 "Not needed". Stays a draft until its visa note (outside India) and emergency numbers are published (1.07 rule 9) |
| **visa-note** | destination, rule text, official link | AI never writes or rewrites this |
| **climate** | destination, best months, source | Entered by hand: Open-Meteo's free API is non-commercial only. Rechecked yearly |
| **festival** | destination, name, dates, year, source | Entered once a year by hand. Calendarific isn't used: its free plan is 500 calls a month with credit required and paid plans start at $12 a month, and a few destinations are quicker by hand |
| **permit-note** | destination, filming rules, drone rules, source | |
| **emergency-number** | destination, number, what it's for, source | Feeds the offline safety card. Never AI-generated |

## Saved views for admin

- "Past 30 days": every entry whose `checkedOn` is more than 30 days ago, in every collection.
- "Draft destinations": destinations not yet published, with which pack items are missing.

## Hindi (phase 2)

Turn on Strapi's localisation for the text fields travellers read (visa rule text, permit notes, emergency "what
it's for", destination notes) in task 2.06. Leave numbers, links and dates unlocalised so they can't drift apart.
