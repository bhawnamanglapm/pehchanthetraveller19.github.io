## Why it matters

The place decides whether the film has a story and whether the trip can earn. The score must come from a formula and
data the traveller can see, never from AI.

## On the page already

The destination list, each with: best months (admin, sourced), UK government travel advice (credited), currency and
languages, ₹ rate with its date, festivals, visa note with "last checked" date and official link, daily cost range,
permit note. Example "why" lines.

## Traveller enters

3–5 candidate places, story angle, scores 0–3 for the questions only they answer, final pick, the "why" line.

## Scoring table

One row per place, one column per question, a total, and **+1 bonus for a place already visited**. Data-filled cells
are marked "from data" with a date. Weights come from trip-page-settings (1.11).

| # | Question | Phase 1 | Phase 2 (2.02, 2.03) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is there a story only I can tell? | Traveller | Same (AI angle ideas in phase 3) |
| 2 | Do Indians want to go there? | Traveller checks Instagram and YouTube | YouTube Data API counts and views |
| 3 | How crowded is it with creators? | Traveller | YouTube counts, refreshed weekly |

Each question has a short "why it matters" line under it, written from README §3. Question 2's line cites its
source: reels and similar posts shape where 44% of Indians travel (Business Standard). Question 8's line says to
publish **before** Indians plan that trip.

Write `score0a.ts` as plain, tested functions.

## Safety and freshness

- The advisory sits beside every place. A serious advisory is flagged on the card, not hidden.
- Any admin fact checked over 30 days ago shows amber: "Last checked over 30 days ago, confirm on the official site."
- A changed advisory shows "Advisory changed on [date]" (1.15).

## Done when, and what carries forward

One place picked and the "why" line written. The "why" goes to the media kit and every pitch (S3 fills from it); the
place and dates go to 0b; the budget goes to P4; `inIndia` sets P3 to Not needed.

## Phase 3

An "Explain" button gives a labelled "AI explanation" of why the top place fits this traveller's budget, month and
angle (3.06). It never changes the score.
