## Why it matters

Browser-only trips are lost when a phone is lost or cleared. Accounts keep a trip safe and on every device.

## What to build

- Sign-in by email link sent through Resend (already in the app). No passwords.
- Accounts stay optional: a traveller can keep using the page with no login, as in phases 1–2.
- On first sign-in, offer to upload the trips in this browser.
- Sync: saves on the device first (offline still works), then syncs when online. Conflicts: the latest change per
  field wins, with a "this trip was changed on another device" note.
- "Delete my account" in the trip menu, which creates a deletion request (3.04).
- Privacy policy and consent screen updated, lawyer-checked.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A trip started offline on a phone appears on a laptop after sync.
- [ ] Not signing in still works exactly as in phase 1.
- [ ] Privacy text updated before release.
