## Why it matters

The brand rests on facts being sourced and dated and on Pehchan-checked never being bought. These rules must hold
even when someone is in a hurry, so the system enforces them rather than relying on memory.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Super admin (owner)** | Approve new destinations, give the creator badge, confirm fake offers, publish showcases (phase 3), publish anything |
| **Editor (helpers)** | Enter and update destination packs as drafts. Can't publish |

First check whether Strapi's free edition allows a custom role that can edit but not publish. If it doesn't, keep the
split by process (editors tell the owner when a draft is ready) and write that in the runbook (1.38).

## Publishing

Every trip collection uses Strapi's Draft & Publish. Nothing reaches the trip app until it's published. Publishing
fires the webhook from 1.05.

## Rules the system enforces (lifecycle hooks or field validation)

1. A fact can't be published without a `source` link and a `checkedOn` date.
2. The creator badge (`openToCreatorStays`) can't be set without the written-OK file and its date.
3. Pehchan-checked has no link to any payment, listing fee or sponsorship field.
4. The consent-form template can't be published without a lawyer-checked date.
5. Tax wording stays hidden until the CA-approved date is set in `trip-page-settings`.
6. A sponsored destination guide (phase 2, task 2.14) is labelled "Sponsored", and the sponsor can't edit its visa or
   safety facts.
7. Hidden links (TripGyani, E3) stay hidden until admin sets them to shown.
8. AI output never enters a Strapi field, except a draft a traveller chooses to send with "Flag this draft"
   (phase 3, `ai-flag` only).
9. A destination stays a draft until its safety items are published: the visa note (outside India) and the
   emergency numbers. The other pack items (1.36) are published when an official source is found; until then they stay
   empty, and the site shows "Not entered yet". **Changed 16 Nov 2026 (owner):** this used to need every item of the
   pack, including 10 checked stays, which would have blocked a launch with no checked stays. The CMS enforces the new
   rule. A cost range that is entered must have both ends and its sources; best months may be left empty if the
   climate note says why and what to avoid.
