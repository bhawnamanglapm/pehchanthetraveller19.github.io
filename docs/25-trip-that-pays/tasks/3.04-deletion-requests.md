## Why it matters

India's data protection law (DPDP Act 2023) gives people the right to have their data erased. Admin must handle it
and keep proof.

## What to build

- `deletion-request` collection in Strapi: account (an internal ID, not the email), date asked, date done.
- "Delete my account" creates the request and signs the traveller out.
- Admin's action deletes the account, trips and files from the traveller database, then sets "date done".
- A confirmation email through Resend.
- Monthly routine item 9 checks the queue.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After deletion, nothing about the account remains in the traveller database or file storage.
- [ ] The Strapi record holds no personal data beyond the internal ID and dates.
