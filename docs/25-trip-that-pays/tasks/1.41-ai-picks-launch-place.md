## Why it matters

The trip pages launch with one destination. **Decided 16 Nov 2026 (owner):** AI picks it, so the launch doesn't wait
for the hand run of stage 0a, which moved to the end of the plan (4.05).

The pick is made by Claude Code in a working session, using web research. That needs no API key and no AI inside the
app, so D6 (₹0 a month, AI off) still holds. AI choosing places for travellers inside the app is a separate, later
feature that needs AI switched on.

## What to do

1. Shortlist 3–5 places worldwide, with the private D3 places as background research only.
2. Score each against the eight stage 0a questions (README §3), weighted toward the launch audience:
   - Safety for women travelling alone.
   - Visa ease for Indian passports.
   - Daily cost.
   - Creator crowding and creator potential.
   - Season: good months in the first months after launch.
3. Give every fact a source link and the date it was checked. A fact without a source is left blank, not guessed.
4. Pick one place and write the "why" as a short paragraph, labelled **AI explanation**.
5. Hand the result to the owner. The owner reads it and either accepts the pick or picks another from the shortlist.

## Rules

- **AI never states visa rules, emergency numbers, prices or rates on its own.** These are copied from the official
  page, linked and dated, and the owner checks them before publishing (1.36).
- **A person checks anything that becomes public.** Nothing goes live until the owner publishes it in Strapi admin.
- **No place name in this public repo** until the destination is published on the site. Until then, the shortlist and
  the scores stay in the private phase 0 workbook.
- The owner's personal travel dates and plans are never part of this task.

## What it doesn't cover

- The destination pack itself: that's 1.36, which uses this pick.
- Checking stays: the launch has none Pehchan-checked (4.06 adds them after launch).
- Checking the pick by hand: 4.05, at the end of the plan.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 3–5 places scored, every fact with a source and a date, kept in the private workbook.
- [ ] One place picked, with an "AI explanation" paragraph.
- [ ] The owner has accepted the pick (or chosen another), with the date.
