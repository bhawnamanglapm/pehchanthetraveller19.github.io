## Why it matters

The trip pages launch with one destination. **Decided 16 Nov 2026 (owner):** AI picks it, so the launch doesn't wait
for the hand run of stage 0a, which moved to the end of the plan (4.05).

The pick is made by Claude Code in a working session, using web research. That needs no API key and no AI inside the
app, so D6 (₹0 a month, AI off) still holds. AI choosing places for travellers inside the app is a separate, later
feature that needs AI switched on.

## What to do

1. Shortlist 3–5 places worldwide, with the private D3 places as background research only.
2. Score each against the eight stage 0a questions (README §3), weighted toward the launch audience:
   - Safety for women travelling alone.
   - Visa ease for Indian passports.
   - Daily cost.
   - Creator crowding and creator potential.
   - Season: good months in the first months after launch.
3. Give every fact a source link and the date it was checked. A fact without a source is left blank, not guessed.
4. Pick one place and write the "why" as a short paragraph, labelled **AI explanation**.
5. Publish the pick and its pack (1.36) in Strapi through its API, without waiting for the owner. The write token
   lives in a local env var only, never in chat or in a repo. **Changed 16 Nov 2026 (owner):** the owner no longer
   reads it first; they check it only if something is found wrong (see below).

## Rules

- **AI never states visa rules, emergency numbers, prices or rates on its own.** These are copied from the official
  page, linked and dated (1.36).
- **Safety facts (emergency numbers, visa, permits) pass automatic checks instead of a person before launch:**
  - Copied only from an official government or embassy page, never from a blog, a travel site or memory.
  - The exact number or rule must appear on the fetched official page, saved with its link and the date.
  - Two official sources must agree. If they don't, or only one exists, the field is left empty, not guessed.
- **Changed 16 Nov 2026 (owner):** a person no longer reads everything before it goes live. "A person checks anything
  that becomes public" now applies as soon as something is found wrong.
- **No place name in this public repo** until the destination is published on the site. Until then, the shortlist and
  the scores stay in the private phase 0 workbook.
- The owner's personal travel dates and plans are never part of this task.

## If something is found wrong

If a traveller, the owner or a later check finds a wrong fact, the owner checks the pack by hand in Strapi admin,
safety facts first (emergency numbers, visa, permits), then the rest. A wrong safety fact is unpublished at once and
fixed after. This check is listed with the people steps (README §16 "Last").

## What it doesn't cover

- The destination pack itself: that's 1.36, which uses this pick.
- Checking stays: the launch has none Pehchan-checked (4.06 adds them after launch).
- Checking the pick by hand: 4.05, at the end of the plan.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] 3–5 places scored, every fact with a source and a date, kept in the private workbook.
- [x] One place picked, with an "AI explanation" paragraph.
- [x] The pick and the pack published in Strapi by the builder, with the date. No owner sign-off (changed 16 Nov 2026).
- [x] Every emergency number, visa and permit field either passed the automatic checks or is empty.
