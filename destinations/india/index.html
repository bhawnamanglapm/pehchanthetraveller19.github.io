Destinations · India

India

A subcontinent of distinct travel countries — Himalayan, coastal, desert, southern — each deserving its own trip.

  1. Home
  2. Destinations
  3. India
Illustrated placeholder

India is treated here as its own region rather than one country in Asia, because it travels like a continent. The mistake most first trips make is covering too much ground; the fix is choosing one of its four travel geographies and staying in it.

Below: every country we cover in India, and every destination guide within them. Each guide follows the same template, so you can compare a Himalayan valley with an Alpine one without re-learning how to read the page.

Munnar

Kerala3 days in Munnar

India · Rajasthan

Udaipur

The lake city of Mewar — palace architecture, Aravalli hills and the most romantic urban setting in India.

Rajasthan3 days for the city

India · Himachal Pradesh

Spiti Valley

A cold desert at 4,000m between the Himalaya and the Tibetan plateau — monasteries, moonscape and very few people.

Himachal Pradesh8–10 days minimum for the loop with proper acclimatisation.

India · Northeast

Meghalaya

The wettest inhabited place on earth — living root bridges, sandstone gorges and Khasi villages in permanent green.

Meghalaya6–7 days for the Khasi and Jaintia hills at a reasonable pace.
