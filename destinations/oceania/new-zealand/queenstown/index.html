Queenstown & Fiordland

An alpine lake town at the edge of the Southern Alps, two hours from the fiords at Milford Sound.

Why visit Queenstown & Fiordland

Queenstown compresses an extraordinary amount of landscape into a short drive: a glacial lake, the Remarkables range straight above the town, vineyards in Gibbston, and Fiordland National Park within a long day trip. It is also the world's most developed adventure-sport town, which means anything you want to try is professionally run — and easy to ignore if you would rather walk.

Best time to visit

  • December to March: long days, warm weather, all trails open.
  • June to August for skiing on four fields within an hour.
  • March to May offers autumn colour and lower prices.
  • Milford Sound is worth visiting in rain — the waterfalls are the point.

How to get there

  • Queenstown (ZQN) has direct flights from across New Zealand and eastern Australia.
  • The drive from Christchurch takes six hours and is a genuine highlight in itself.
  • Rent a car for Fiordland and Wanaka; the town centre is walkable.

How many days do you need

4–5 days for Queenstown and Milford; 10+ for a South Island loop.

Where to stay

Queenstown centre for restaurants and lake access, Arrowtown for a quieter historic base, Glenorchy at the head of the lake for real isolation.

Best things to do in Queenstown & Fiordland

  • Walk or ride the Queenstown Hill and Ben Lomond tracks for the lake from above.
  • Drive to Glenorchy along the lake road — one of the world's best short drives.
  • Take a Milford Sound cruise, ideally overnighting in Te Anau to avoid the 12-hour day.
  • Cycle the Gibbston River trail between wineries.
  • Book a Routeburn or Kepler day section if you cannot do the full Great Walk.

Food

  • Central Otago pinot noir, from vineyards twenty minutes away.
  • Bluff oysters in season, from March.
  • Green-lipped mussels and crayfish on the coast.
  • Excellent coffee — New Zealand takes it as seriously as Australia.

Budget

Queenstown is New Zealand's most expensive town. Wanaka and Te Anau are meaningfully better value bases, and self-catering apartments help considerably.

Safety & practical information

  • Alpine weather changes fast; check the forecast and register intentions for longer walks.
  • Sandflies in Fiordland are relentless — repellent is essential, not optional.
  • UV levels are among the highest in the world; sunburn happens quickly even in cloud.

Local culture

  • This is Ngāi Tahu territory; place names and interpretive signage are increasingly bilingual.
  • Freedom camping is regulated and enforced — use designated sites.
  • Leave gates as you find them on farm-crossing trails.

Frequently asked questions

Is Milford Sound a good day trip from Queenstown?

It is possible but long — roughly twelve hours including the cruise. Staying overnight in Te Anau splits the drive and lets you take an early, quieter cruise.

How many days do you need in Queenstown?

Four to five: two for the town and its walks, one for Glenorchy, one for Milford or Wanaka, plus a weather buffer.

Is Queenstown only for adventure sports?

No. The bungy and jet boats are famous, but the hiking, wineries, lake drives and Fiordland day trips stand entirely on their own.
