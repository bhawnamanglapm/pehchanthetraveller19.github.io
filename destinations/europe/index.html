Destinations · Europe

Europe

Short distances, deep history and some of the most walkable cities and dramatic mountain valleys anywhere.

  1. Home
  2. Destinations
  3. Europe
Illustrated placeholder

Europe is the easiest continent to travel slowly. Rail networks make multi-country journeys practical, and the difference between a good trip and a great one is usually the choice of base town rather than the number of countries.

Below: every country we cover in Europe, and every destination guide within them. Each guide follows the same template, so you can compare a Himalayan valley with an Alpine one without re-learning how to read the page.

Italy · Campania

Amalfi Coast

Vertical villages above the Tyrrhenian — lemon terraces, ferry hops and a coast road that is best not driven.

Italy4–5 days, or 7 combined with Naples or Capri.
Illustrated placeholder
Portugal · Atlantic coast

Lisbon

Seven hills, Atlantic light and a city that has changed fast without losing its tiled, tram-rattled character.

Portugal3–4 days for the city, 6–7 with Sintra and the coast.
Illustrated placeholder
Switzerland · Bernese Oberland

Lauterbrunnen Valley

A glacial trough with seventy-two waterfalls and car-free villages hung on the cliffs above it.

Switzerland3–4 days minimum
