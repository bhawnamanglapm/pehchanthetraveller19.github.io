Asia

From Thailand's islands to Vietnam's limestone bays — the region most Indian travellers reach first, and the one that rewards a second visit most.

  1. Home
  2. International
  3. Asia
Illustrated placeholder

Asia is the easiest first international trip from India: short flights, visa-on-arrival in much of it, and a cost of travelling that leaves room to stay longer. We cover it as places worth several days, not as country checklists.

Below: every country we cover in Asia, and every destination guide within them. Each guide follows the same template, so you can compare a Himalayan valley with an Alpine one without re-learning how to read the page.
