Path of the Gods Guided Walk

The classic Bomerano-to-Nocelle traverse, high above the coast on an old mule path, ending with the long stair descent into Positano. Walked east to west, downhill overall, with a guide who knows where the terraces and the ruins are.

What it is

The classic Bomerano-to-Nocelle traverse, high above the coast on an old mule path, ending with the long stair descent into Positano. Walked east to west, downhill overall, with a guide who knows where the terraces and the ruins are.

Ideal traveller

Reasonably fit walkers who want the coast from above rather than from the road.

What is included

  • Guide
  • Bus transfer up to Bomerano
  • Water and a packed snack
  • Descent to Positano

What to expect

  • Around 8km, mostly downhill, ending with roughly 1,700 steps.
  • Proper walking shoes are essential — this is not a sandal route.
  • Little shade; start early in summer.
  • Not suitable for anyone with knee problems on the descent.
