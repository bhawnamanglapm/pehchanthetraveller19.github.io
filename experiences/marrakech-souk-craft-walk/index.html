Souk Craft Trades Walk

A morning through the medina's trade quarters with a guide from a craft family — dyers, tanners, metalworkers, woodturners — visiting workshops that make rather than sell, with no commission stops.

What it is

A morning through the medina's trade quarters with a guide from a craft family — dyers, tanners, metalworkers, woodturners — visiting workshops that make rather than sell, with no commission stops.

Ideal traveller

Travellers who want the souks as workshops rather than as shops.

What is included

  • Guide from a medina craft family
  • Four to five workshop visits
  • Mint tea
  • Medina orientation

What to expect

  • Explicitly no commission shops — this is the point.
  • The tanneries have a strong smell; mint is provided.
  • Narrow lanes and uneven ground.
  • Purchases can be arranged directly with makers afterwards.
