Sintra Before the Crowds

What it is

A deliberately early Sintra day: first entry at Pena, then the Moorish castle walls and Quinta da Regaleira in the afternoon, structured to be one step ahead of the coaches all day.

Ideal traveller

Anyone who has heard Sintra is overcrowded and wants it done properly.

What is included

  • Transport from Lisbon
  • Pre-booked timed entries
  • Guide
  • Lunch in Sintra village

What to expect

  • Very early start — this is the entire point.
  • Considerable walking on hills and cobbles.
  • Palace interiors are busy by mid-morning regardless.
  • Returns to Lisbon by 6pm.
