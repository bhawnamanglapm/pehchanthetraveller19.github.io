Meghalaya · Meghalaya

Double-Decker Root Bridge Trek

Down roughly three thousand stone steps from Tyrna to Nongriat, across two steel wire bridges to the double-decker living root bridge grown from rubber fig roots over generations, with the river pools below.

What it is

Down roughly three thousand stone steps from Tyrna to Nongriat, across two steel wire bridges to the double-decker living root bridge grown from rubber fig roots over generations, with the river pools below.

Ideal traveller

Fit walkers; overnight strongly recommended over the day version.

What is included

  • Local Khasi guide
  • Village entry fees
  • Water
  • Porter arrangement if required

What to expect

  • 3,000 steps down and the same back up — genuinely strenuous.
  • Steps are slippery when wet; avoid in heavy monsoon.
  • Overnight in the village transforms the experience.
  • Leeches are common in the wet months.
