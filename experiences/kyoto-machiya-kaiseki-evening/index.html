Machiya Kaiseki Evening

A seasonal multi-course kaiseki dinner in a converted townhouse, hosted by the chef, with each course explained in terms of the season, the region it came from and why it appears in this order. Twelve seats, one sitting a night.

What it is

A seasonal multi-course kaiseki dinner in a converted townhouse, hosted by the chef, with each course explained in terms of the season, the region it came from and why it appears in this order. Twelve seats, one sitting a night.

Ideal traveller

Couples and food-led travellers who want the seasonal Kyoto meal explained rather than simply served.

What is included

  • Eight-course seasonal kaiseki
  • Sake or tea pairing
  • Chef's explanation of each course in English
  • Small-group seating, maximum twelve

What to expect

  • Arrive on time — the first course is plated to the minute.
  • Courses follow the season rather than a fixed menu.
  • Dietary requirements need at least 72 hours' notice.
  • The meal runs roughly three hours; do not book anything after it.
