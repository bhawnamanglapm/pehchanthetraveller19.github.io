Wadi Rum · Jordan

Zarb Dinner & Desert Night

Watch a zarb oven — meat and vegetables buried in the sand over coals — opened at dusk, then eat it around the fire while the guides explain the Bedouin calendar and the protected area's management.

What it is

Watch a zarb oven — meat and vegetables buried in the sand over coals — opened at dusk, then eat it around the fire while the guides explain the Bedouin calendar and the protected area's management.

Ideal traveller

Families and first-time desert visitors.

What is included

  • Zarb dinner
  • Bedouin tea and coffee
  • Fire and music
  • Stargazing

What to expect

  • The oven is opened at sunset; arrive before.
  • Cold after dark year-round; bring layers.
  • Vegetarian versions cooked separately with notice.
  • Usually combined with an overnight camp stay.
