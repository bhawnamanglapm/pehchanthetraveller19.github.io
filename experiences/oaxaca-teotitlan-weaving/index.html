Teotitlán Natural Dye Workshop

What it is

A morning with a Zapotec weaving family in Teotitlán del Valle, working with cochineal, indigo and marigold on the loom, and understanding why a genuine hand-woven rug costs what it costs.

Ideal traveller

Families and craft travellers.

What is included

  • Transport from Oaxaca
  • Dye demonstration and hands-on session
  • Loom instruction
  • Lunch with the family

What to expect

  • Hands-on and suitable for children over eight.
  • Dyes stain — wear clothes you do not mind.
  • No purchase obligation, but buying direct matters here.
  • Roughly 45 minutes' drive each way.
