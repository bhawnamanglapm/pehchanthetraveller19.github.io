Nishijin Weaving Morning

A morning in the Nishijin textile district with a working weaver, covering the obi looms, the dyeing houses and why this eight-block area produced the fabric of the imperial court for five hundred years.

What it is

A morning in the Nishijin textile district with a working weaver, covering the obi looms, the dyeing houses and why this eight-block area produced the fabric of the imperial court for five hundred years.

Ideal traveller

Travellers who have already seen the main temples and want the working city.

What is included

  • Working atelier visit
  • Loom demonstration
  • Walk through the dyeing lanes
  • Tea with the weaver

What to expect

  • Small group, maximum eight.
  • Photography permitted in the atelier but not of individual designs.
  • Comfortable shoes — this is a walking morning.
  • No purchase pressure; commissions can be arranged separately.
