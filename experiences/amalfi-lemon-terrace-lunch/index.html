Lemon Terrace Lunch

Lunch on a working lemon terrace above the coast with the family that farms it — how the pergola system works, why the sfusato lemon only grows here, and a long table meal built around what the terrace produced that week.

What it is

Lunch on a working lemon terrace above the coast with the family that farms it — how the pergola system works, why the sfusato lemon only grows here, and a long table meal built around what the terrace produced that week.

Ideal traveller

Couples and food travellers wanting the working agricultural coast.

What is included

  • Terrace walk with the grower
  • Four-course lunch
  • Local wine
  • Limoncello tasting

What to expect

  • Steps up to the terrace — moderate mobility needed.
  • Roughly three and a half hours; nothing is rushed.
  • Vegetarian menus available with notice.
  • Runs March to October only.
