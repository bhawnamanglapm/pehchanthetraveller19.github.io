Schilthorn Ridge Walk

What it is

Cable car to the Schilthorn, then the ridge descent past Birg toward Mürren with the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau across the valley the entire way. Guided, because the weather here turns faster than the forecast suggests.

Ideal traveller

Confident walkers who want the high route rather than the cable car round trip.

What is included

  • Mountain guide
  • Cable car up
  • Route planning and weather assessment
  • Safety equipment as required

What to expect

  • Alpine terrain — snow patches possible into July.
  • Roughly 1,300m of descent; poles strongly recommended.
  • Rescheduled without charge if the weather closes in.
  • Not suitable for anyone uncomfortable with exposure.
