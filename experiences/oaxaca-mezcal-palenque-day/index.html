Mezcal Palenque Day

Out to two family palenques in the valleys, following agave from the field through the roasting pit and the horse-drawn tahona to the still — and tasting varietals that never leave the state.

What it is

Out to two family palenques in the valleys, following agave from the field through the roasting pit and the horse-drawn tahona to the still — and tasting varietals that never leave the state.

Ideal traveller

Anyone who thinks of mezcal as a bar drink and is about to stop.

What is included

  • Transport
  • Two family palenque visits
  • Tastings
  • Lunch in a valley village

What to expect

  • Tastings are substantial; do not drive afterwards.
  • Producers are working families, not visitor centres.
  • Roughly two hours of driving in total.
  • Bottles can be bought directly from the makers.
