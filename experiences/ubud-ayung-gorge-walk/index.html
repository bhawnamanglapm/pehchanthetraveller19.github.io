Ubud · Indonesia

Ayung Gorge & Rice Terrace Walk

Down into the Ayung river gorge and back up through working rice terraces with a guide from the subak irrigation cooperative, who explains the thousand-year-old water-sharing system that shapes every terrace you see.

Location
Ubud, Indonesia
Duration
4 hours
Difficulty
Moderate — steep sections
Recommended time
Early morning, 6.30am

What it is

Down into the Ayung river gorge and back up through working rice terraces with a guide from the subak irrigation cooperative, who explains the thousand-year-old water-sharing system that shapes every terrace you see.

Ideal traveller

Walkers who want Ubud's landscape before the day heats up.

What is included

  • Local guide from the subak cooperative
  • Water and fruit
  • Village temple visit
  • Hotel pick-up in the Ubud area

What to expect

  • Steep, uneven and slippery after rain — proper shoes required.
  • Roughly 7km with 200m of descent and ascent.
  • Leeches are possible in wet months.
  • Finishes before 11am, when the heat arrives.
