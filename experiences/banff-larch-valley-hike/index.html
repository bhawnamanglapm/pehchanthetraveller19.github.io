Larch Valley & Sentinel Pass

From Moraine Lake up through the larch bowl to Sentinel Pass at 2,611m, timed for the fortnight in late September when the larches turn gold and the Ten Peaks sit behind them.

What it is

From Moraine Lake up through the larch bowl to Sentinel Pass at 2,611m, timed for the fortnight in late September when the larches turn gold and the Ten Peaks sit behind them.

Ideal traveller

Fit walkers visiting in the two-week larch window.

What is included

  • Guide
  • Moraine Lake shuttle booking
  • Bear spray
  • Group size limits per Parks Canada requirements

What to expect

  • Around 12km with 725m of ascent.
  • Parks Canada may require groups of four or more in this area for bear safety.
  • Moraine Lake shuttle must be booked well ahead.
  • Snow is possible at the pass from September.
