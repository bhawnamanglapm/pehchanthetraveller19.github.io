Maasai Mara · Kenya

Dawn Game Drive

Out of camp before first light with a Maasai guide, into the conservancy while the predators are still active, with coffee stopped for wherever the morning takes you.

Location

Duration

Difficulty

Recommended time

What it is

Out of camp before first light with a Maasai guide, into the conservancy while the predators are still active, with coffee stopped for wherever the morning takes you.

Ideal traveller

Anyone on safari — the first drive of the day is the best one.

What is included

  • Guide and spotter
  • 4x4 vehicle
  • Bush coffee stop
  • Conservancy fees where applicable

What to expect

  • Very early start — the first two hours are the productive ones.
  • Cold at dawn; blankets provided.
  • Sightings are never guaranteed and should not be promised.
  • Usually included in all-inclusive camp rates.

Reviews

We do not publish reviews or ratings we have not collected ourselves, and we do not republish a partner's star rating as though it were ours. When first-party reviews exist for this experience, they will appear here with the collection method stated. Until then, the honest answer is: we have none.
