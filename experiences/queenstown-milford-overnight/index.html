What it is

An overnight small-ship cruise into Milford Sound, anchoring in a side cove after the day boats leave — which is when the fiord becomes what people travel here for.

Ideal traveller

Travellers who want Milford without the twelve-hour day trip.

What is included

  • Overnight berth
  • Dinner and breakfast
  • Kayaks or tender excursion
  • Nature guide

What to expect

  • Rain is normal and improves the waterfalls significantly.
  • Sandflies are relentless on deck; repellent essential.
  • Cabins are compact — this is a small ship.
  • Te Anau is a better departure base than Queenstown.
