Offering-Making with a Family

A morning in a family compound learning to make canang sari — the daily palm-leaf offerings placed across Bali — followed by the household temple visit they are made for, with the family explaining the calendar behind it.

What it is

A morning in a family compound learning to make canang sari — the daily palm-leaf offerings placed across Bali — followed by the household temple visit they are made for, with the family explaining the calendar behind it.

Ideal traveller

Travellers who want to understand Balinese daily practice rather than watch a performance.

What is included

  • Materials
  • Instruction from the household
  • Sarong and sash
  • Household temple visit
  • Tea and snacks

What to expect

  • This is a family home, not a venue — dress and behave accordingly.
  • Sarong and sash are required and provided.
  • Photography is permitted except during prayer.
  • Menstruating women are traditionally asked not to enter temple areas; the family will explain.
