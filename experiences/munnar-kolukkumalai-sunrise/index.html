Munnar · Kerala

Kolukkumalai Sunrise Jeep

A pre-dawn jeep up a rough track to Kolukkumalai, among the highest tea estates in the world, arriving above the cloud layer for sunrise and staying for the estate's own hand-rolled processing.

What it is

A pre-dawn jeep up a rough track to Kolukkumalai, among the highest tea estates in the world, arriving above the cloud layer for sunrise and staying for the estate's own hand-rolled processing.

Ideal traveller

Early risers who want the high ranges above the cloud line.

What is included

  • 4x4 jeep and driver
  • Estate factory visit
  • Tea tasting
  • Chai at the summit

What to expect

  • The track is genuinely rough — an hour of hard jeep driving.
  • Cold before sunrise; bring a warm layer.
  • Clouds can obscure the view entirely; it is a gamble.
  • Back at the hotel for a late breakfast.
