Moray & Maras Half Day

What it is

The circular agricultural terraces at Moray, where the Inca ran microclimate experiments across twenty distinct temperature zones, followed by the pre-Inca salt pans at Maras still worked by local families.

Ideal traveller

Travellers acclimatising in the valley before Machu Picchu.

What is included

  • Transport
  • Guide
  • Site entries
  • Water

What to expect

  • Around 3,500m — take it slowly if newly arrived.
  • Strong sun and cold wind at the same time.
  • Salt pan walkways are narrow and slippery.
  • Pairs well with an afternoon at the hotel.
