Hoi An · Vietnam

Market-to-Table Cooking Class

Start at the central market with a cook who buys there daily, cross the river by basket boat to a garden kitchen, and cook four central-Vietnamese dishes including cao lau, which exists only in this town.

What it is

Start at the central market with a cook who buys there daily, cross the river by basket boat to a garden kitchen, and cook four central-Vietnamese dishes including cao lau, which exists only in this town.

Ideal traveller

Families and first-time visitors who want to understand central Vietnamese cooking.

What is included

  • Market tour with a working cook
  • Basket boat crossing
  • Four-dish cooking session
  • Lunch of what you cooked
  • Recipe notes

What to expect

  • Early start to beat the heat and get the best produce.
  • Vegetarian and vegan versions available with notice.
  • Suitable for children over eight.
  • You will eat a full lunch — plan the afternoon lightly.
