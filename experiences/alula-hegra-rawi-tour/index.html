Hegra with a Rawi Guide

The official guided route through Hegra's Nabataean tombs with a rawi storyteller, covering the four main tomb clusters including Qasr al-Farid, and why a city of this size sat at the southern edge of the Nabataean kingdom.

What it is

The official guided route through Hegra's Nabataean tombs with a rawi storyteller, covering the four main tomb clusters including Qasr al-Farid, and why a city of this size sat at the southern edge of the Nabataean kingdom.

Ideal traveller

Anyone visiting AlUla — this is the site the destination exists for.

What is included

  • Site entry
  • Rawi guide
  • Vehicle between tomb clusters
  • Water

What to expect

  • Independent access is not permitted; this is the only way to see the site.
  • Book well ahead in the December–February season.
  • Very little shade — go early or late.
  • Roughly three hours across a wide site.
