Hoi An · Vietnam

Rice Field & Fishing Village Cycle

A flat cycle out through Tra Que herb village, along the paddy bunds and down to a working fishing village on the estuary, timed for either the morning harvest or the evening light.

Location
Hoi An, Vietnam
Duration
4 hours
Difficulty
Easy — flat throughout
Recommended time
Early morning or late afternoon

What it is

A flat cycle out through Tra Que herb village, along the paddy bunds and down to a working fishing village on the estuary, timed for either the morning harvest or the evening light.

Ideal traveller

Anyone comfortable on a bicycle who wants the countryside rather than the old town.

What is included

  • Bicycle and helmet
  • Guide
  • Herb village visit
  • Refreshments at a village house
  • Estuary boat crossing

What to expect

  • Entirely flat — no cycling fitness required.
  • Roughly 15km at a slow pace with frequent stops.
  • Sun protection is essential in the middle of the day.
  • Best booked for 6.30am or 3.30pm.
