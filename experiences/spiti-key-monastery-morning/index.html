Spiti Valley · Himachal Pradesh

Key Monastery Morning Prayers

Early morning at Key Gompa, the largest monastery in Spiti, for the monks' prayer session, followed by butter tea and an explanation of the monastery's thousand-year history from a resident monk.

Location
Spiti Valley, Himachal Pradesh
Duration
3 hours
Difficulty
Easy — high altitude
Recommended time
From 6am

What it is

Early morning at Key Gompa, the largest monastery in Spiti, for the monks' prayer session, followed by butter tea and an explanation of the monastery's thousand-year history from a resident monk.

Ideal traveller

Travellers already acclimatised in Kaza.

What is included

  • Transport from Kaza
  • Monastery donation
  • Butter tea
  • Introduction from a resident monk

What to expect

  • Prayers are a practice, not a performance — enter quietly.
  • Photography restricted during prayers; ask first.
  • 3,800m — move slowly.
  • Bitterly cold in the early morning even in summer.
