Lake Pichola Sunset Boat

A boat across Lake Pichola in the last light, past the City Palace waterfront and Jag Mandir, which is the angle the whole city was built to be viewed from.

What it is

A boat across Lake Pichola in the last light, past the City Palace waterfront and Jag Mandir, which is the angle the whole city was built to be viewed from.

Ideal traveller

Everyone visiting Udaipur — the city was designed to be seen this way.

What is included

  • Boat seat
  • Jag Mandir island stop
  • Guide commentary

What to expect

  • Book the last departure of the day for the light.
  • Boats are shared unless you charter privately.
  • Water levels are lowest in May and June before the monsoon.
  • Roughly 90 minutes including the island stop.
