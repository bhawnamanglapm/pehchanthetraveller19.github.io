Ethical Elephant Sanctuary Day

A full day at a sanctuary that does not offer riding, bathing or performances — observation, feeding preparation and time with the mahouts explaining each animal's history and the economics that make genuine sanctuaries difficult to run.

What it is

A full day at a sanctuary that does not offer riding, bathing or performances — observation, feeding preparation and time with the mahouts explaining each animal's history and the economics that make genuine sanctuaries difficult to run.

Ideal traveller

Families and travellers who want to see elephants without riding or bathing them.

What is included

  • Return transfers from Chiang Mai
  • Lunch
  • Sanctuary entry and contribution
  • Mahout-led explanation

What to expect

  • No riding, no bathing, no performances — this is deliberate.
  • Observation distances are maintained for the animals' welfare.
  • Long day with roughly two hours of driving in total.
  • Suitable for children; walking on uneven ground.
