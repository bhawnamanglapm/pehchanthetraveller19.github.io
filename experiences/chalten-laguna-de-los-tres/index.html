El Chaltén & Patagonia · Argentina

Laguna de los Tres Trek

The full day walk from El Chaltén to the glacial lake beneath Fitz Roy, easy for the first 8km and brutally steep for the last one, ending at one of the great mountain amphitheatres.

What it is

The full day walk from El Chaltén to the glacial lake beneath Fitz Roy, easy for the first 8km and brutally steep for the last one, ending at one of the great mountain amphitheatres.

Ideal traveller

Fit walkers with a full day and a flexible schedule.

What is included

  • Guide
  • Route and weather assessment
  • Packed lunch
  • Trekking poles if needed

What to expect

  • Around 20km return with 750m of ascent, most of it in the last kilometre.
  • Patagonian wind is constant; the summit section can be dangerous in gusts.
  • No permit required, which is unusual and welcome.
  • Guides will postpone rather than walk into bad weather.
