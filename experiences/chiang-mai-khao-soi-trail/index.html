Northern Thai Food Trail

Six stops across the old city and Chang Phueak, focused on Lanna dishes — khao soi, sai ua, nam prik ong — with a guide who explains why northern Thai food is a separate cuisine.

What it is

Six stops across the old city and Chang Phueak, focused on Lanna dishes — khao soi, sai ua, nam prik ong — with a guide who explains why northern Thai food is a separate cuisine.

Ideal traveller

Solo travellers and couples who want the northern kitchen, not Bangkok street food.

What is included

  • Six tasting stops
  • All food and one drink per stop
  • Songthaew between districts
  • Guide

What to expect

  • Small group, usually six to ten people.
  • Spice levels can be adjusted; some dishes cannot be made vegetarian.
  • You will finish full — skip dinner.
  • Ends near the night bazaar for onward plans.
