Wadi Rum · Jordan

Jebel Burdah Rock Bridge

A guided scramble to the Burdah rock bridge, one of the highest natural arches in the world, with genuine exposure on the upper section and a view across the whole protected area from the top.

What it is

A guided scramble to the Burdah rock bridge, one of the highest natural arches in the world, with genuine exposure on the upper section and a view across the whole protected area from the top.

Ideal traveller

Confident scramblers with a head for heights.

What is included

  • Bedouin guide
  • 4x4 transfer to the base
  • Rope protection on the exposed section
  • Lunch and water

What to expect

  • Real exposure — this is not a walk.
  • Roped where necessary, but you must be comfortable on rock.
  • Not suitable for children or anyone with vertigo.
  • Cancelled in high wind.
