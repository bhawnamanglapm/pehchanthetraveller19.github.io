Gharameel Stargazing

An evening in the Gharameel rock field — sunset among the sandstone pinnacles, then a guided sky session as the desert dark comes in, with Bedouin tea and dinner around a fire.

What it is

An evening in the Gharameel rock field — sunset among the sandstone pinnacles, then a guided sky session as the desert dark comes in, with Bedouin tea and dinner around a fire.

Ideal traveller

Photographers and anyone who has never seen a genuinely dark sky.

What is included

  • Transfers from AlUla
  • Guide and astronomy session
  • Dinner and tea
  • Blankets

What to expect

  • Genuinely cold after dark from November to February.
  • Best around the new moon; full-moon nights lose the faint objects.
  • Roughly 45 minutes' drive each way.
  • Telescope availability varies by operator.
