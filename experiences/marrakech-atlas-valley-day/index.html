Marrakech · Morocco

High Atlas Valley Day

Into the Imlil or Ourika valley for a walk between Amazigh villages, lunch with a family, and a look at how the mountain communities live an hour from Marrakech — with snow on the peaks above from December to April.

What it is

Into the Imlil or Ourika valley for a walk between Amazigh villages, lunch with a family, and a look at how the mountain communities live an hour from Marrakech — with snow on the peaks above from December to April.

Ideal traveller

Anyone who wants mountains and a Berber village lunch within a day of the city.

What is included

  • Transfers from Marrakech
  • Local mountain guide
  • Village lunch
  • Mule support where needed

What to expect

  • Roughly 3–4 hours of walking at an easy pace.
  • Modest dress in villages.
  • Altitude of around 1,800m — noticeable but not difficult.
  • Long day, back in Marrakech by 6pm.
