Let's create better journeys together

We work with hotels, resorts, tourism boards, airlines, tour operators, experience providers and travel brands on storytelling that a reader actually finishes.

  1. Home
  2. Partner

Pehchan is a global travel discovery platform: destination guides, stays, experiences, curated journeys and a trip planner, built for travellers in the research phase — the point at which a recommendation still changes a decision.

We are early. We would rather tell you that than quote numbers we cannot yet stand behind. What we can offer today is a well-built, fast, editorially serious platform, a defined audience, transparent labelling of every paid placement, and a team that will produce work you would be happy to run on your own channels.

What we will not do: publish a paid placement without labelling it, write a review we do not believe, invent ratings or awards, or promise editorial coverage in exchange for a rate.

Partnership FAQs

Do you guarantee positive coverage?

No. Sponsored placements are guaranteed placement, production quality and disclosure — not a guaranteed verdict. If we cannot recommend something honestly, we will tell you before the work starts rather than write around it.

How is sponsored content labelled?

Every paid placement carries a visible Sponsored badge at the point of the content, not only in a footer policy. Affiliate links carry their own disclosure on the same page as the link.

Do you accept free stays in exchange for coverage?

We will accept a hosted stay to make a review possible, and we disclose it on the page. It does not buy a positive review, and it does not guarantee publication.

What audience data can you share?

We will share current analytics directly with prospective partners rather than publish traffic claims on a marketing page. We would rather under-promise on a call than over-claim in public.

Do you work with early-stage brands?

Yes. Tell us the budget honestly and we will tell you what is realistic within it.
