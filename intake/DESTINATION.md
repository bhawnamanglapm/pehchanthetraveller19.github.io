## What this place asks of a traveller

**The single most important section, and the one nothing else can replace.**

The profile engine matches a woman's comfort against what a place actually
demands. Right now no destination has these scores, so `/profile/` cannot
answer "can I do this?" for anywhere. Twelve axes, scored 1 to 5 for the
**place**, not for you:

> 1 = not required · 2 = rarely needed · 3 = sometimes · 4 = often · 5 = unavoidable

| Axis | Score | The question to ask yourself |
|---|---|---|
| Flying alone | | Can she realistically get here without flying? |
| Trains | | Is a train the normal way in? |
| Buses | | Is a bus unavoidable on any leg? |
| Driving | | Does getting around need her own vehicle? |
| Taxis | | Will she be alone in a car with a stranger? |
| Being out after dark | | Does anything here require her out at night? |
| Hostels | | Is dorm accommodation the realistic option? |
| Homestays | | Will she be staying in someone's home? |
| Eating alone | | Will she eat by herself, visibly, in public? |
| Meeting strangers | | Does the trip require talking to people she doesn't know? |
| Changing places | | How often does she pack and move? |
| No connectivity | | Will she lose signal, and for how long? |

Score what the place **demands**, honestly. A 5 on "eating alone" is not a
criticism of the place — it means a woman who dreads it will find this hard,
and she deserves to know before she books.
