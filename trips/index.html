Rishikesh, Uttarakhand

3 days · from Delhi · Alone · first solo trip

✓ Trip verified

Went out feeling nervous — came back confident.

What I wish I knew before going
The bus drops you a long way from where the hotels actually are, and the last stretch is a shared auto that fills up slowly. I would have booked the train and arranged a pickup.
Planned
₹12,000
Actually spent
₹14,200
Something went wrong — Transport

My bus was two hours late and I arrived after dark with no pickup arranged.

What she did: Waited inside the terminal until I could book a cab through the hotel rather than taking one from the rank.

Her advice: Book arrival before 5pm. If you are running late, wait somewhere staffed rather than solving it on the street.

Her advice to the next woman

Book the train, not the bus, and tell the hotel your arrival time so somebody is expecting you. That one message changed the whole trip for me.

Amritsar, Punjab

2 days · from Chandigarh · Alone · first solo trip

Community experience

Went out feeling very nervous — came back neutral.

What I wish I knew before going
That going very early is not only about the crowd. It is quiet, and being there alone at that hour felt completely different from the afternoon.
Planned
₹8,000
Actually spent
₹7,600
Something went wrong — Booking

The hotel had no record of my booking when I arrived.

What she did: Showed the confirmation on my phone; they found a room. I had also screenshotted it in case there was no network.

Her advice: Screenshot every confirmation. Do not rely on the email loading when you need it.

Her advice to the next woman

Go at 5am. Screenshot everything. And do not book the expensive room out of nervousness — the ordinary one was fine.

How this works

Checked by a person, published within 12 hours

You write it
Whatever you are willing to share. Every field is optional except the destination and how long you went for.
A person reads it
Not an algorithm. We check it reads like somebody who actually went, and we look for anything that would identify you by accident.
We check the evidence, then destroy it
If you attach a booking or ticket, it earns a Trip verified mark. We confirm it and delete the file — we do not keep your documents.
It goes up within 12 hours
Or you hear back explaining why not. Nothing is published silently and nothing is edited beyond spelling without asking you.
You can take it down
Any time, no reason needed, and it is gone from the site the same day.
Your safety

How your story is protected

Anonymous by default
Your name is never shown unless you specifically ask for it. Most women choose anonymous, and that is the default.
We never publish exactly where you stayed
Not with your dates. A property plus a date range plus a lone woman is an identification, so one of the three is always removed.
Naming a business follows a separate route
If something happened to you at a named hotel or with a named driver, that goes through a private incident report rather than a public post — so it can be checked and answered before anyone reads it.
Harassment and assault are handled off the page
Never published as a story. Tell us privately and it informs what we warn other women about, without your experience becoming public content.
We hold as little as possible
Verification documents are destroyed after checking. Contact details are separated from your story, and are only used to reach you about it.
